# Spec RL-12: Pulse run cards and a health scorecard

## Status
Approved after review round 4 (`reviews/RL-12/r4-approved.md`). Revision 5 applies its suggestions
S19–S23 (wording only). Revision 4 answered round 3, revision 3 round 2 (and made the health window
include `now`), revision 2 round 1.

Source: [harness efficiency and insights](../analysis/2026-11-19-harness-efficiency-and-insights.md),
§6.5 (revised core), item I-2 (pulse health and run cards). Builds on RL-3 (pulses, slots, overlap),
RL-2 (review records, fold, items), RL-4 (debriefs), RL-6 (calibration), RL-9 (sinks, clean-audit
goals) and RL-11 (runs, trial goals, `/healthz` features). Release v0.1.31.

## Problem

1. **A pulse's history is spread over five reads.** `GET /v1/pulses/:id` lists slots and the state
   of their goals. To show "what each run of this pulse did" (which stage waited, how long, what it
   cost, what it raised, what people decided, where the findings were filed, the report to read),
   a client reads each goal, its steps, its review records, its items, its dispositions and its
   artifacts, and finds the clean-audit goals itself. Nobody can compare a run with the previous one
   without that join.
2. **No overview.** "Are the pulses healthy this week?" needs slots due against slots that ran,
   why the others did not, what holds a pulse, what the runs cost and wasted, what was accepted, and
   which debriefs and findings wait on people. Today that is a join over every pulse, slot, goal,
   step, run, disposition and item, done by every client.

## Scope

In:
- `GET /v1/pulses/:id/runs`: one **run card** per slot of a pulse, newest first, paged.
- `GET /v1/health`: a **scorecard** per pulse and a total, over a window of days.
- Two new names in `/healthz` `features`: `health` and `pulse-runs`.
- Store reads that keep both routes off full scans of slots (below).

Out:
- A composite score (efficiency, waste) and derived ratios other than the one named
  (`costPerAcceptedUsd`): the hub reports counts and sums; a client divides them.
- CLI commands for the new routes (a later release may add `mesh pulse runs` and `mesh health`).
- CI or code-quality data (the hub records no CI; a later release records the commit per round).
- Subagent cost (I-22) and time per session state (I-15).
- OpenMetrics `/metrics` (I-14).
- Any change to slots, goals, steps, items or events: both routes only read.

## Interface contracts

### Common rules

- Rule `member`, service scope `goals:read`, as `GET /v1/pulses/:id`. Like that route, neither is
  wrapped for a person: a service that sends `x-mesh-on-behalf-of` gets 400 `on-behalf-of is not
  accepted on this route` (`docs/guide/auth.md`); a service reads with its own token.
- Both routes read in one store transaction and write nothing.
- Times are milliseconds since the epoch. `now` is the hub clock at the read.
- An unknown query parameter is ignored.

**Trial.** `trial` query: `exclude` (default) or `include`; `GET /v1/pulses/:id/runs` also takes
`only`. Another value → 400 `trial must be exclude, include or only` (runs) or `trial must be exclude
or include` (health). A trial slot has `trial: true`; a trial goal has `trial: true` (RL-11). What
`trial` filters, and only that:
- which **slots** are listed or counted (cards, `manual`, `lastSlot`); scheduled slots are never
  trial slots (a trial fire is a manual slot);
- which **goals** are the pulse's goals (`goals`, `runs`, `accepted`, `debriefs`, and the goals whose
  raising records name the lineages of `findings`).

Item states, the card's `items` and `sinks`, and the counts of `findings` are **lineage-level**: a
trial run's record folds, files and sinks like any other (RL-11), so they include what trial runs did
on the lineage's items. `blockedBy` ignores `trial` (trial goals never block, RL-11).

### Stage times (shared by cards and `debriefs`)

For a step `s` of goal `G`:
- A dep's **first end**: the `finishedAt` of its first round with `outcome: done`; else, for a dep in
  state `skipped` or `dropped`, its `updatedAt` (a replan rewrites it, so after a replan this is the
  replan time); else none.
- **`readyAt`** (the first cycle): when `s` has deps, the latest first end over them, present only
  when every dep has one. When `s` has no deps: the `decidedAt` of the oldest approved plan of `G`
  that lists `s`'s local id (for a pulse goal, plan v1, approved at `createdAt`). Absent otherwise.
- **`startedAt`**: the first round's `startedAt`; when `s` has no rounds and is `claimed` or `paused`,
  `s.claimedAt`; else absent.
- **`waitMs`**: `startedAt − readyAt` when both are present (0 when negative); else absent. It is the
  wait of the first cycle.
- **`cycleReadyAt`** (a later cycle): when `s` is `ready`, `claimed` or `paused`, let `t` be the
  latest of `s`'s own newest round's `finishedAt` (when it has rounds), the `finishedAt` of every round
  of any step of `G` (moved-away steps included) whose `reopen` lists `s.id` (compared without case), and each dep's newest `done`
  round's `finishedAt`. `cycleReadyAt` = `t` when `t` exists and is later than `readyAt` (or `readyAt`
  is absent); else absent. This covers a step reopened directly, the failing step that waits for its
  reopened dep, and a dependant (with or without rounds) whose dep was reopened and done again. For a
  step held through a rejected or parked round, `t` is that round's end although the step did not
  wait; the guide says so.
- **`waitingMs`**: when `s` is `ready`: `now − (cycleReadyAt ?? readyAt)` (0 when negative), absent
  when neither is present; else absent. It is how long the step has been waiting now.

### Types (src/protocol/hub.ts, exported from src/protocol/index.ts)

`HUB_FEATURES` becomes `["health", "pulse-runs", "runs", "trial"]`.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `step` | the full step id |
| `kind` | `step.kind` |
| `mode` | `step.policy.mode`, else `interactive` |
| `state` | `step.state` |
| `holder` | `step.holderUser` when the step is `claimed` or `paused`; else absent |
| `agent` | the `telemetry.agentKey` of the step's newest round with telemetry; else `step.policy.agent`; else absent |
| `rounds` | the number of rounds |
| `outcome` | the newest round's `outcome` (also a rejected round's `fail` while the step is still claimed); absent without rounds |
| `readyAt`, `startedAt`, `waitMs`, `cycleReadyAt`, `waitingMs` | as in *Stage times* |
| `finishedAt` | the newest round's `finishedAt`; absent without rounds |

**`RunCard`**: one slot.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `slot`, `at`, `state`, `decidedAt` | as stored on the slot |
| `reason`, `by`, `running`, `error`, `firedAt`, `until`, `startedAt`, `goal` | as stored, absent when the slot has none (`goal` is the goal id, also when that goal no longer exists) |
| `manual`, `trial`, `catchUp` | `true` when the slot has the mark, else `false` |
| `tries` | the number of entries in the slot's `tried` |
| `startWaitMs` | when the slot has `startedAt`: `startedAt − firedAt` for a manual or catch-up slot, `startedAt − at` otherwise (0 when negative); else absent |
| `goalTitle`, `goalState`, `goalCreatedAt` | the goal's `title`, `state` and `createdAt` when the slot names a goal that exists; else absent |
| `stages` | the goal's steps as `StageCard`s, in the order `GET /v1/goals/:id` lists them, moved-away steps left out (`[]` without a goal) |
| `totals` | `{runs, durationMs, turns, costUsd?}`: the sums of the stages' fields (`costUsd` absent when no stage has one) |
| `fold` | for each step of the goal, the `fold` of its newest round that has one; the sums of those (`new`, `repeat`, `regression`, `resolved`, `carried`); absent when no round has a fold |
| `items` | when the goal has raising review records: `{raised, open, confirmed, accepted, dismissed, deferred, resolved}`, where `raised` is the number of distinct `(lineage, fingerprint)` pairs over the items of all the goal's raising records (new and repeated items alike) and each other count is how many of those items are in that state **now**; else absent |
| `dispositions` | `{confirmed, dismissed, deferred, accepted, byPeople, reverted}`: over the lineages of the goal's raising records, the disposition rows (`DispositionRow`, as `GET /v1/items` history lists them) whose `goal` is this goal, by disposition; `byPeople` how many of them a person decided; `reverted` the number of `revert` history entries in this goal on those lineages. Every row counts, also one reverted later. These rows can concern items an earlier goal raised (a carried item decided here), so they need not match `items`. All 0 without raising records. |
| `reports` | the ids of the goal's stored artifacts of kind `report`, in upload order, pruned ones included (`[]` without) |
| `sinks` | the clean-audit goals holding a live finding step for an item **filed in the goal** (the RL-9 term): `[{goal, repo, topic, steps}]`, ordered by goal id (below); `[]` without |
| `delta` | the change against the previous complete run (below); absent when there is none |

`sinks`: the items filed in the goal are, over the lineages of the goal's raising records, the items
the sink would add for this goal (`filedItems(lineage, goal)`: the item is `accepted` with a `link`
now and its latest `accepted` disposition was recorded in this goal). For each, its live finding steps
are those of `findingStepsOf` (clean-audit goals that are not dropped, `step.finding` matches, not
moved away). `steps` is the number of such steps in a clean-audit goal `C`; `repo` and `topic` are
`C.sink`'s. A clean-audit goal with none is not listed. An item filed here and reverted, or filed
again later in another goal, is not in this goal's `sinks`.

`delta`: the previous complete run is the newest slot older than this one (in card order, below),
among at most the 50 slots that follow it, whose goal exists and is `done` and that is a trial slot
exactly when this one is. It exists only when this card's goal is `done`. `delta` = `{slot, goal,
durationMs, turns, costUsd?, raised, new, regression}`, each number being this card's value minus the
previous one's: `durationMs` and `turns` from `totals`, `raised` from `items.raised`, `new` and
`regression` from `fold` (absent `items` or `fold` count as 0). `costUsd` is present only when both
cards have `totals.costUsd`. `slot` and `goal` name the previous run. `trial` does not change which
slot is the previous run.

**`RunCardsPage`**: `{pulse, cards: RunCard[], next?: string}`.

### `GET /v1/pulses/:id/runs`

| Parameter | Meaning | Invalid → 400 with |
|---|---|---|
| `trial` | `exclude` (default), `include` or `only` (trial slots only) | `trial must be exclude, include or only` |
| `limit` | 1–100, default 20 | `limit must be a whole number from 1 to 100` |
| `cursor` | the `next` of a previous page | `cursor is not valid` |

- Unknown or deleted pulse → 404 `no pulse <id>` (as `GET /v1/pulses/:id`).
- Cards: every slot of the pulse that passes `trial`, ordered as `GET /v1/pulses/:id` orders slots
  (`at` descending, then `slot` descending, as binary strings). `next` is present when more cards
  follow; passed back as `cursor` it returns the cards after the last one returned (the cursor is a
  position `(at, slot)`, so it stays valid with another `trial`). `next` is opaque. A slot has a card
  whatever its state.

### `GET /v1/health`

| Parameter | Meaning | Invalid → 400 with |
|---|---|---|
| `days` | 1–90, default 7: the window is `[now − days × 86 400 000, now]` (both ends included) | `days must be a whole number from 1 to 90` |
| `repo` | only pulses whose `params.repo` equals it (the repo the pulse runs in, not the target repos of its findings) | — |
| `topic` | only pulses whose `topics` contain it (trimmed and lowercased; `all` matches every pulse) | — |
| `trial` | `exclude` (default) or `include` | `trial must be exclude or include` |

Reply `Health`: `{window: {since, until, days}, pulses: PulseHealth[], total: HealthTotal}`, with
`until` = `now`. `pulses`: every pulse that is not deleted and passes `repo` and `topic`, ordered by
the number in its id.

The pulse's **goals** are the goals with `refs.pulse` = its id (trial goals left out with
`exclude`). A slot is **in the window** when its `at` is.

**`PulseHealth`**:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pulse`, `title`, `owner`, `enabled`, `schedule`, `pausedReason`, `nextAt` | as `GET /v1/pulses` shows the pulse (`pausedReason` and `nextAt` absent when it has none) |
| `slots` | over the pulse's scheduled (not manual) slots in the window: `{due, fired, started, skipped, missed, abandoned}`. `due` = their number; `fired`, `skipped`, `abandoned` = how many are in that state now; `missed` = `{<reason>: n}` for each reason of their `missed` slots (`{}` when none); `started` = how many have `startedAt` (whatever their state now, so an abandoned slot that had started counts in both). |
| `manual` | the number of the pulse's manual slots in the window that pass `trial` |
| `lastSlot` | the newest slot of the pulse that passes `trial` (card order): `{slot, at, state, reason?}`; absent when none |
| `blockedBy` | below; absent when nothing blocks |
| `runs` | over the rounds with telemetry of the pulse's goals' steps whose `finishedAt` is in the window: `{count, durationMs, turns, costUsd?, failed, overBudget, denials, allDismissed}`; `durationMs`, `turns` and `denials` are sums, `costUsd` the sum of the estimates present (absent when none), and `failed`, `overBudget`, `allDismissed` the number of runs whose RL-11 `waste` part of that name is `true`. `count` equals the number of rows `GET /v1/runs?pulse=<id>&since=<since>&until=<until + 1>&trial=<trial>` returns over all its pages. |
| `goals` | over the goals of the pulse's slots in the window that pass `trial` (manual included): `created` their number, and `done`, `dropped`, `open` how many are in that state now (`open` = any other state) |
| `accepted` | the number of distinct items `(lineage, fp)` that are `accepted` now and whose latest `accepted` disposition row (whether or not it was reverted later) was recorded in one of the pulse's goals with `at` in the window (an item accepted by a debrief and then linked by a filer counts once; an acceptance reverted to a state other than `accepted` does not count, because the item is not `accepted` now) |
| `costPerAcceptedUsd` | `runs.costUsd / accepted`, rounded to 4 decimals; absent when `accepted` is 0 or `runs.costUsd` is absent. Both parts are by time in the window: the runs that finished and the acceptances recorded in it. |
| `debriefs` | the steps with `mode: debrief` of the pulse's goals (any age) whose goal is not `done` or `dropped` and whose state is `ready`, `claimed` or `paused`: `{open, oldestMs?}`; `oldestMs` is the largest `now − (cycleReadyAt ?? readyAt)` among them (0 when negative), absent when none has either time |
| `findings` | over the items of every lineage that a raising review record of the pulse's goals names (all time): `{open: {S1,S2,S3,S4}, accepted: {S1,S2,S3,S4}}`, `open` counting items in state `open` or `confirmed`, `accepted` those in state `accepted` (with or without a link) |

**`blockedBy`**: when the pulse's `overlap` is `skip`, what `fireSlot` would find at `now`, read
only. Over the pulse's `fired` slots in card order, a slot's goal is passed over when it does not
exist, is `done` or `dropped`, or is a trial goal. A slot is **started** when it has `startedAt` or
`slotStarted(goal, run step)` holds (the test `advanceSlot` applies before it retires a slot: the goal
is `done`, or the run step is headless and `done`, `failed` or has reported `run.started`, or it is
not headless and `claimed`, `paused`, `done` or `failed`). A goal is also passed over when its slot is
not started and `now ≥ until` (the fire would retire it), or when its slot is started and `now −
goal.createdAt ≥ maxGoalAgeMs` (the fire would mark it started, then abandon it). The first other goal for which `overlapBusy(steps,
runStepOf(goal))` names a step gives `{goal, step, ageMs}` (`ageMs` = `now − goal.createdAt`).
Absent with `overlap: allow` or when no goal blocks.

**`HealthTotal`**: `{pulses, slots, manual, runs, goals, accepted, costPerAcceptedUsd?, debriefs,
findings, calibrations}`: `pulses` the number listed; `slots`, `manual`, `runs`, `goals`, `accepted`
and `debriefs.open` the sums over the listed pulses (`missed` per reason; `costUsd` absent when absent
on every pulse); `debriefs.oldestMs` the largest; `costPerAcceptedUsd` from the totals as for a pulse;
`findings` over the union of the listed pulses' lineages (an item counts once); `calibrations` =
`{open, oldestMs?}` over the hub's calibration goals (goals with `calibration`) not `done` or
`dropped`, whatever `repo`, `topic` or `trial` say, `oldestMs` = `now − createdAt` of the oldest.

### Reads (implementation contract)

- Slots: `listSlots` gains `before?: {at, slot}` (strictly after that position in card order) and
  `since?: number` (`at ≥ since`), both in SQL; a card page reads `limit + 1` slots after the cursor,
  plus at most 50 more for `delta`. Health reads the slots in the window per pulse, plus the `fired`
  slots for `blockedBy`, plus slots from the newest until the first that passes `trial` for
  `lastSlot`.
- Health `runs` reads steps with `listSteps({updatedSince: since})` (as `GET /v1/runs`).
- Dispositions and reverts are read per lineage from the item history (indexed on `lineage`) and
  grouped by goal, never with a `goal` filter (which scans the history table); `listDispositions`
  leaves out revert entries, so the read returns every entry with its fingerprint.
- Health `findings` reads the raising records once (`listReviews({raising: true})`) and the items of
  each named lineage once (`listItems`).
- The guide says that a health call reads every raising record and is meant for a page load, not a
  poll every few seconds.
