# Spec RL-14: Time per session state and a goal efficiency card

## Status
Approved after review round 3 (`reviews/RL-14/r3-approved.md`). Revision 4 applies its suggestions
S13–S18. Revision 3 answered review round 2 (`reviews/RL-14/r2-changes-requested.md`: B2 the
held rule, B3 the mark persisted on the session record, S9–S12). Revision 2 answered round 1 (B1
`notHeld`, S1–S8).

Source: [harness efficiency and insights](../analysis/2026-11-19-harness-efficiency-and-insights.md),
item I-15 (time per session state, goal efficiency card, stuck signal), SHOULD in §6.5 row 7. §6.3
places session states among the harness-native facts the hub owns. Builds on M4 (session states,
`session.*` events, step facts), RL-7 (a running headless run's sessions are never stalled), RL-11
(trial goals, `/healthz` features), RL-12 (run cards, `goals:read` read routes without on-behalf) and
RL-13 (the commit of each round). Release 0.1.36.

## Problem

1. **Time waiting on a person is not kept.** The daemon knows each session's state (`working`,
   `needs_you`, `idle`, `stalled`) and sends `session.waiting` and `session.turn_*` events for
   sessions that hold a step. The hub can only guess durations from those events (step facts:
   `waits.minutes`, `activeMinutes`): a wait ends at the next `turn_started`, idle and stalled time is
   not counted at all, events are pruned after the retention period, and a window cannot be cut out
   of them reliably.
2. **No efficiency view of a goal.** Turns, wall time, waits, fix cycles, parks and take-overs exist
   in parts (step facts, rounds, the ledger roll-up written when a goal is done), but no read shows
   them together for a goal or a step while it runs, or summed over a window.
3. **Nobody is told when work is stuck.** A session that waits for a person for hours, or a step
   that keeps turning without landing a commit, is visible only to someone who looks at the session.

## Scope

In:
- The daemon accumulates **milliseconds per session state, per session and step, per UTC day**, and
  sends **absolute totals** (never increments) with its sync. The hub stores them per `(step, sid)`.
- `GET /v1/goals/:id/efficiency`: an **efficiency card** for a goal and each of its steps (all time).
- `GET /v1/efficiency`: the same figures **summed over a window** (`days`, or `since`/`until`), per
  goal, per repo and in total, with `repo` and `trial` filters.
- `GET /v1/stuck`: the **stuck signal**: sessions in `needs_you` longer than a threshold, and claimed
  steps with many turns since their last commit; a per-repo roll-up.
- `Session.stateSince` (when the session entered its current state), synced with the session record.
- `efficiency` in `/healthz` `features`.
- Migration: schema version 12 (a `step_time` table and an index on `event.at`).

Out:
- A composite score or ratio (owner rule; §6.1 C-5). The hub returns parts; a client divides.
- A new event, or an event per state transition. No event is added or changed.
- Time of a session while it holds no step: it stays on the machine (plan Q5); only time spent
  holding a step is sent.
- CLI commands for the new routes, OpenMetrics, pushed roll-ups (I-14, I-18).
- CI or code-analysis data (the hub keeps none; RL-13's commit is the join key).
- Back-filling state time for rounds that ended before the upgrade, or from older daemons.
- Changes to session state rules (`stallAfterMs` and the other time rules are unchanged).

## Interface contracts

### Units and words

- Durations are whole milliseconds, named `*Ms`. Times are milliseconds since the epoch. `now` is the
  hub clock at the read. `D` = 86 400 000.
- **State time** of a session is the time it spent in each of `working`, `needs_you`, `idle` and
  `stalled` while it **held a step** (`Session.step` set). `ended` and a placeholder window (`launching`)
  never accrue. A **day** is a UTC day `[d, d + D)` with `d` a multiple of `D`.
- `needsYouMs` is the time the agent waited for a person (a permission prompt, a question, a dialog,
  input). `idleMs` and `stalledMs` are time nobody was working on it (a turn ended and no prompt
  followed; `stalled` is idle beyond `stallAfterMs`, never for a running headless run's session,
  RL-7). `workingMs` is the active time.

### Daemon: accumulating (src/core/state-time.ts, pure)

`Session` (src/protocol/session.ts) gains two optional fields:
- `stateSince?: number`: when the session entered its current `state`. Synced with the record.
- `stateTime?: {since: number; steps: Record<string, StateDay[]>; notHeld?: string[]}`: local only. `syncedSessions`
  drops it from every session record a sync sends, and the hub's `sync` deletes it before
  `upsertSession`, so it is never stored on the hub or returned by `/v1/live` or `/v1/fleet` (only the
  daemon's own `/local/sessions` shows it). `since` is the start of the open interval; `steps` maps a
  full step id to its closed day totals, days ascending; `notHeld` lists the step ids the hub said
  this session does not hold (below). The daemon store keeps it with the record, so it survives a
  restart.

`StateDay` (src/protocol/hub.ts) = `{day, workingMs, needsYouMs, idleMs, stalledMs}`: `day` a
multiple of `D`; each field a whole number `0 … D`; their sum `≤ D`.

A session **accrues to step S** when `step === S`, its state is one of the four, it is not
`launching`, and `(sid, S)` is not marked **not held** (below).

`accrue(prev: Session | undefined, next: Session, now): Session` (the mark is read from the records) returns `next` with:
- `stateTime.since = now`, and `stateTime.steps` = `prev.stateTime.steps` plus, when `prev` has
  `stateTime` and accrued to `prev.step`, the interval `[prev.stateTime.since, now)` added to
  `steps[prev.step]` in the bucket of `prev.state`, **split at each UTC midnight**. An interval of
  negative length (a clock that went back) adds nothing, and each field of a day is clamped so the
  day's sum stays `≤ D` (a clock that jumps back and forth never makes a day the hub refuses). Without
  `prev` or without `prev.stateTime`: `steps` = `next`'s or `{}`, and nothing is added (accrual
  starts at the first change after the upgrade).
- When `next` accrues to `next.step`, `steps[next.step]` exists (`[]` when new), so a step's first
  open interval is reported before any other change.
- `stateSince` = `now` when there is no `prev` or `prev.state !== next.state`; else
  `prev.stateSince` (which stays unset for a session that was already in its state at the upgrade:
  such a session is `unmeasured` in `/v1/stuck` until its state changes).
- Bounds: a step keeps its newest 400 days; a session keeps its 50 steps with the newest last day
  (a step with no day yet counts as newest).

The daemon applies `accrue` to every changed session in `commit` (hooks, observations, ticks,
launches, run sessions, `update` from tool calls) and in `markWorking`, with `deps.now()`. So every
change closes the open interval and the stored record holds closed totals plus `since`.

**Live totals.** `liveDays(s, step, now)` = `s.stateTime.steps[step]` plus, when `s` accrues to
`step`, `[since, now)` split at midnight in the bucket of `s.state` (clamped as above).

**Not held (a step taken over, dropped, skipped, moved or finished elsewhere).** The daemon clears
`Session.step` only on its own `mesh_done`/`mesh_release` and a few hub messages, so a session whose
step another session took over, or that a person dropped, keeps `step` set and would keep accruing
(mostly `stalled`) time. To stop that without changing who holds what:
- **Held rule** (modelled on the check `runs.ts` makes before closing a run's step, which tests the machine and the
  holder session only): step `S` is held by session
  `sid` of the calling user `u` on machine `m` when `S` is `claimed` or `paused` and either
  `S.holderSid === sid`, or `S.holderSid` is absent, `S.holderUser === u` and `S.holderMachine` is
  absent or `m`. (A CLI take, `POST …/take` with `{}`, and a headless run's accept clear
  `holderSid`; such a step stays held by its user's sessions on that machine, so a running headless
  run never loses its time, and the old session of a CLI take by another person is not held.)
- The `POST /v1/sync` reply gains `notHeld?: {sid, step}[]`: for each session record of the body that
  has `step` and is stored (owned by the caller), an entry when the hub's step (looked up without
  case) is unknown or is not held by that session under the held rule. The field is present (maybe
  `[]`) exactly when the body has a session record with a `step`.
- The daemon records the reply on the session records: for each session **in that body** with a
  step, `stateTime.notHeld` gains the step when the reply lists `(sid, step)` and loses it when it
  does not. Sessions not in the body (beyond the body's cap of 200) keep their marks. Each change
  goes through `accrue` as a session change: adding a mark closes the open interval as held (the lag
  is at most one sync, 5 s by default, or the time the daemon was offline), removing one closes it as
  not held (adds nothing) and restarts accrual at `now`. A session accrues to `S` only when `S` is not
  in its `stateTime.notHeld`.
- `stateTime.notHeld` only ever names the session's current step: `accrue` drops a mark for any
  other step (a session that moved to another step starts unmarked). The reply's `step` is the
  spelling the daemon sent. The marks are applied to the daemon's current session records through
  `commit` (not to the copy the flush read), so hook changes made during the sync are kept.
- A successful reply without `notHeld` (an older hub) leaves the marks as they are. When the link's
  credentials name another hub or machine, every mark is cleared before the next sync (the new hub's
  first reply sets them again), so a session never stays marked by a hub it no longer talks to.
- When a flush makes several `/v1/sync` calls (one per 200 outbox events), the **first** body carries
  the `stateTime` entries (and their 500 cap) and the marks are taken from the **first** successful
  reply.
- `notHeld` changes nothing else: the session keeps `step`, its tools and the autopilot behave as
  before. An older hub sends no `notHeld`: the daemon accrues as long as `step` is set.

**Daemon restart.** Sessions are loaded with `stateTime`. The first change after the restart closes
`[since, now)` into the state the session had when the daemon stopped: daemon downtime counts as
that state (the guide says so).

### Daemon: sending (src/daemon/hub-link.ts, src/daemon/daemon.ts)

`SyncRequest` gains `stateTime?: StateTimeReport[]` (at most 500), each
`{sid, step, asOf, days: StateDay[]}` (1 … 400 days, `day` strictly ascending, `asOf ≥` the last
`day`). `asOf` is the daemon clock when the sync body was built; `days` are the **absolute** live
totals `liveDays(s, step, asOf)` of that `(sid, step)`. The hub keeps the latest, so a retried,
duplicated or replayed sync never adds anything twice.

An entry is **open** when its session accrues to its step now, else **closed**. The daemon remembers,
per key `(sid, step)`, the last entry the hub accepted: `{open, state, asOf, days}` (in memory; reset
when the link's credentials name another hub or machine, as `factsFor` resets the other sent facts).
A sync carries every key in `stateTime.steps` of a session in memory for which:
1. nothing was accepted (since the daemon started, or since the hub changed); or
2. the entry's `open`, or the session's `state`, differs from the remembered one; or
3. it is open and the remembered `asOf` is at least 60 000 ms old (`STATE_TIME_EVERY_MS`); or
4. it is closed and its `days` differ from the remembered ones.

A key whose live `days` are empty (nothing accrued yet) is not sent and not marked. Open entries
first, then the others by `sid`, `step`; at most 500 per sync; the rest go in the next
sync. An entry counts as accepted when the sync call returns 2xx; on a failed call nothing is marked,
and the next sync recomputes the entries with the newer clock. The outbox and the offline queue are
not involved: state time is never queued, only recomputed.

An ended session leaves the daemon's memory after 24 hours (as today) only once each of its keys
was accepted with its final `days`; otherwise it stays until they are, at most 7 days (the daemon
store's session retention). After a restart nothing is remembered, so every key of every session in
memory is sent once.

### Hub: storing (src/hub/service.ts `sync`, src/store/hub-store.ts)

Migration 12 (both dialects; the coordinator may renumber it):
`step_time (step TEXT NOT NULL, sid TEXT NOT NULL, goal TEXT NOT NULL, user_id TEXT NOT NULL,
machine_id TEXT NOT NULL, as_of <int> NOT NULL, first_day <int> NOT NULL, value TEXT NOT NULL,
PRIMARY KEY (step, sid))`, indexes on `goal` (Postgres: `lower(goal)`) and `as_of`; and
`CREATE INDEX event_at ON event (at)`. `value` is the JSON `days` array. `mesh hub migrate` (copyHubToPostgres, `TABLES` in src/store/hub-copy.ts) copies the
table.

In `POST /v1/sync`, after the session records, for each `stateTime` entry, in order:
1. The entry is parsed alone (`StateTimeReport`); an invalid one is skipped. An invalid entry never
   fails the sync (events and sessions in the same body are stored as before). A body with more than
   500 entries, or `stateTime` that is not an array, is a 400 like any other invalid body.
2. The step is looked up as `getStep` does (without case); unknown → skipped. The row is stored
   under the step's own id and its `goal`.
3. Skipped when the hub has a session row for `sid` owned by another user, or a `step_time` row for
   `(step, sid)` owned by another user.
4. Upsert `(step, sid)` with `user_id` = the caller, `machine_id` = the token's machine, `as_of`,
   `first_day` = the first `day`, `value`; on conflict the row is replaced **only when** the stored
   `as_of ≤` the new `asOf` (one SQL statement). An older entry never overwrites a newer one.

`SyncResponse` gains `notHeld?: {sid, step}[]` (above). No event is written.

### Common rules of the three routes

- Rule `member`, service scope `goals:read`, as RL-12: not wrapped for a person, so a service that
  sends `x-mesh-on-behalf-of` gets 400 `on-behalf-of is not accepted on this route`.
- Each reads in one store transaction and writes nothing. An unknown query parameter is ignored.
- `trial`: `exclude` (default) or `include`; another value → 400 `trial must be exclude or include`.
  It filters goals by `goal.trial` (RL-11). `GET /v1/goals/:id/efficiency` takes no `trial`.
- **Absent, not zero:** `stateTime` is absent on a step that has no `step_time` row (no session
  reported state time: an older daemon, a step never held by a session of a 0.1.36 daemon, or held
  before the upgrade). A goal or roll-up has `stateTime` when at least one of its steps has, and
  `stateTimeSteps` says how many of its steps contributed, so a client can tell partial coverage.

`StateTime` = `{workingMs, needsYouMs, idleMs, stalledMs}`.

**Step figures** (`StepFigures`), shared by the card (all time) and the window (inside the window):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `stateTime` | sums over the step's `step_time` rows (window: apportioned, below); absent without rows |
| `turns` | the number of `session.turn_started` events of the step (window: with `at` in it) |
| `rounds`, `fails`, `parks`, `commits` | the step's rounds (window: with `finishedAt` in it); of them, `outcome: fail`; with `parked`; with `commit` |
| `takeOvers` | the number of `step.taken_over` events of the step (window: in it) |
| `runs`, `runDurationMs`, `runTurns`, `costUsd` | over those rounds with `telemetry`: their number, the sums of `durationMs` and `turns`, the sum of `costUsdEstimate` where present (absent when none has) |
| `done`, `firstTryPass` | card: booleans; window: 0 or 1. `done`: the step is `done` now (window: and its newest round, `outcome: done`, finished in it). `firstTryPass`: `done`, `fixCycles` 0 and no round with `outcome: fail` |

`turns` counts prompts (a session's turns, from hooks); `runTurns` counts the agentic turns a
headless run reported. They are different units and are never added. Event-based counts cover the
hub's event retention only (`events_retention_days`); the guide says so.

### `GET /v1/goals/:id/efficiency`

Unknown goal → 404 `no goal <id>` (as `GET /v1/goals/:id`). Reply `GoalEfficiency`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `goal`, `title`, `state`, `createdAt` | the goal's |
| `trial` | `true` for a trial goal, else `false` |
| `endAt` | when the goal is `done` or `dropped`: the latest `endAt` of its steps, else (no step has one) the goal's `updatedAt`; else absent |
| `wallMs` | `(endAt ?? now) − createdAt` (0 when negative) |
| `stateTime`, `stateTimeSteps` | sums over the steps' `stateTime`; `stateTimeSteps` the number of steps with one |
| `turns`, `rounds`, `fails`, `parks`, `commits`, `takeOvers`, `runs`, `runDurationMs`, `runTurns`, `costUsd` | sums over the steps (`costUsd` absent when absent on every step) |
| `fixCycles` | the sum of the steps' `fixCycles` (every reopen starts a fix cycle) |
| `steps` | the number of steps; `done` how many are `done`; `firstTryPass` how many have `firstTryPass` |
| `sessions` | `SessionTime[]` summed per `sid` over the goal's rows: `{sid, user, machine (the machine id), asOf (the newest), workingMs, needsYouMs, idleMs, stalledMs}`, ordered by `sid` |
| `stepCards` | `StepEfficiency[]`, every step of the goal (moved-away, dropped and skipped ones included: time spent there is real), in the order `GET /v1/goals/:id` lists steps |

`StepEfficiency` = `StepFigures` (card form) plus:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `step`, `kind`, `repo`, `state`, `fixCycles` | the step's |
| `mode` | `policy.mode`, else `interactive` |
| `holder` | `holderUser` when `claimed` or `paused`; else absent |
| `movedTo` | the step's `movedTo` when set |
| `startedAt` | the first round's `startedAt`; else `claimedAt` when `claimed` or `paused`; else absent |
| `endAt` | the newest round's `finishedAt` when the step is `done` or `failed`; its `updatedAt` when `dropped` or `skipped` and `startedAt` is present; else absent |
| `wallMs` | `(endAt ?? now) − startedAt` (0 when negative) when `startedAt` is present; else absent |
| `commit`, `commitAt` | the `commit` and `finishedAt` of the newest round with a commit; absent when none |
| `sessions` | `SessionTime[]`: one per `step_time` row of the step, ordered by `sid` (`[]` without rows) |

### `GET /v1/efficiency`

| Parameter | Meaning | Invalid → 400 with |
|---|---|---|
| `days` | 1–90: the window is `[m − (days − 1) × D, now + 1)` where `m` is today's UTC midnight (whole UTC days up to now) | `days must be a whole number from 1 to 90` |
| `since`, `until` | as in `GET /v1/runs` (milliseconds or ISO time); the window is `[since, until)`; `until` defaults to `now + 1`, `since` to `until − 7 × D` | RL-11's texts; `since must be before until`; `the window is at most 90 days` |
| (none of them) | `days=7` | — |
| `repo` | only steps whose `repo` equals it | — |
| `trial` | as above | `trial must be exclude or include` |
| `steps` | `1`: each goal row carries `stepRows` | — |

`days` with `since` or `until` → 400 `days cannot be combined with since or until`.

**Apportioning.** A `step_time` row contributes, per day bucket `[d, d + D)` of its `days`, the part
of the bucket inside the window: the bucket's time happened in `[d, e)` with `e = min(d + D, as_of)`;
the fraction is `|[d, e) ∩ W| / (e − d)` (when `e ≤ d`: 1 if `d ∈ W`, else 0), and each field adds
`round(field × fraction)`. Time is assumed spread evenly inside its bucket. A window whose edges are
UTC midnights (every `days` window, at its start; any `since`/`until` at midnight) takes whole buckets
and is exact; the day containing `now` is cut at `as_of`, which is the latest it can hold.

A step is **in the window** when it passes `repo`, its goal passes `trial`, and it has at least one of:
a bucket with a non-zero fraction, a round with `finishedAt` in the window, a `session.turn_started`,
`step.reopened` or `step.taken_over` event with `at` in the window. Its row has `StepFigures` (window
form) plus `step`, `repo`, `reopens` (its `step.reopened` events in the window).

Reply `EfficiencyWindow`:
- `window`: `{since, until}` (the resolved bounds; `until` exclusive).
- `total`: `Rollup` over every step in the window.
- `repos`: `[{repo, ...Rollup}]` per step `repo`, ordered by `repo`.
- `goals`: `[{goal, title, state, trial, ...Rollup, stepRows?}]` for each goal with a step in the
  window, ordered by the number in the goal id, newest first; `stepRows` (with `steps=1`) its steps in
  the window in goal order.

`Rollup` = `{steps, stateTime?, stateTimeSteps, turns, rounds, fails, parks, commits, takeOvers,
reopens, runs, runDurationMs, runTurns, costUsd?, done, firstTryPass}`: `steps` the number of steps in
the window, the rest sums (`stateTime` and `costUsd` absent when absent on every step).

Unpaged: a 90-day window lists at most the goals of 90 days. The guide says a call reads every step
and `step_time` row of the window and the events in it, and is meant for a page load, not a poll.

### `GET /v1/stuck`

| Parameter | Meaning | Invalid → 400 with |
|---|---|---|
| `needsYouMs` | 60 000 – 604 800 000, default 1 800 000 (30 min) | `needsYouMs must be a whole number from 60000 to 604800000` |
| `turns` | 1 – 10 000, default 40 | `turns must be a whole number from 1 to 10000` |
| `repo`, `trial` | as above (by the step's repo and goal) | as above |

Reply `Stuck`: `{thresholds: {needsYouMs, turns}, sessions, steps, repos, unmeasured}`.
- `sessions` (**per session**): hub sessions that are listed, `needs_you`, hold a step that exists
  and is held by the session under the held rule (with the session row's user and machine; a session
  left on a step another session took over is not listed), and passes `repo` and `trial`, have `stateSince`, and
  `now − stateSince ≥ needsYouMs`: `{sid, user, machine (the machine id), step, goal, repo, needs?,
  since, forMs, lastSeenAt}`, by `forMs` descending, then `sid`. `lastSeenAt` is when the machine
  last synced the record (the session row's `updated_at`): a machine that stopped syncing keeps a
  growing `forMs`, and `lastSeenAt` shows it. `unmeasured`: the number of sessions that pass every
  other test but have no `stateSince` (an older daemon, or a session already waiting at the upgrade);
  they are not listed.
- `steps` (**per step**): steps in state `claimed` that pass `repo` and `trial` whose
  `turnsSinceCommit` ≥ `turns`. `turnsSinceCommit` = the step's `session.turn_started` events with
  `at` after the `finishedAt` of its newest round with a `commit` (all of them when none has):
  `{step, goal, repo, holder, turns, commit?, commitAt?, claimedAt?}`, by `turns` descending, then
  `step`.
- `repos`: `[{repo, sessions, steps}]` for each repo with at least one listed entry, ordered by repo.

Why these units: `needs_you` belongs to one session (one prompt on one machine waits for one person);
a step has at most one live holder session and earlier sessions of the step cannot be waiting. Turns
and commits belong to the step: turns add up across its sessions, take-overs and fix cycles, and
commits are recorded on its rounds (RL-13), so "many turns, no commit" is only measurable per step.
A repo roll-up counts both, by the step's repo.

### Compatibility

- **Migration: yes** (schema 12: `step_time`, `event_at`). Additive; no existing row changes.
- A 0.1.36 daemon with an older hub: `SyncRequest` is not strict, so `stateTime` and
  `Session.stateSince` are dropped; nothing fails.
- An older daemon with a 0.1.36 hub: no `stateTime`, no `stateSince`: `stateTime` absent on its
  steps (not zero), its needs-you sessions counted in `unmeasured`. Turns, rounds and every other
  figure work as for any step.
- A client detects the routes by `efficiency` in `/healthz` `features`.
