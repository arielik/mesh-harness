## Problem

1. **No goal membership.** "Join" today means taking a step (`mesh claim`, the client's spin-up). A person who
   follows a goal, reviews its findings or owns its outcome has no record of that on the hub, so the client cannot
   show "My goals". The client's owner chose a hub record (D1), not a client-side list.
2. **A done goal cannot grow.** A report's findings, reviewed after the last step finished, become new steps. On
   an `in_progress` goal an amendment does this today. On a `done` goal `proposePlan` refuses with `409`
   (src/hub/service.ts:2874), `replan` refuses with `409 goal-closed` (:2840), and the work has to move to a new
   goal, which splits one piece of work across two goals.
3. **The list read cannot say what waits.** `GET /v1/live` gives each goal `done`, `total` and `holders`
   (src/hub/service.ts:3881). It does not say how many steps are parked or failed, whether a plan waits for
   approval on an `in_progress` goal, or whether a findings review (a debrief step) waits. The client's Goals list
   needs all three for every goal in one read; one `GET /v1/goals/:id` per goal is too many reads.

## Terminology

- **Member**: a hub user recorded on a goal by a join and not removed by a leave.
- **Join / leave**: the person's own write that adds or removes their membership.
- **Amendment**: a plan proposed while the goal already has an approved plan (`in_progress`, or `done` after this
  spec).
- **Reopen**: a `done` goal leaving `done` because new work was added (an approved amendment, or a replan).
- **Findings review**: a step whose `policy.mode` is `debrief`.

## Scope

In:
- A `goal_member` table, backfilled with the owner of each open person-origin goal (REQ-GOALMEM-12); join and
  leave routes, member and act-for; `goal.joined` and `goal.left` events.
- The person who creates a goal is its first member.
- `members` and `planPending` in `GET /v1/goals`, `members` in `GET /v1/goals/:id`; `member` and `state` filters on
  `GET /v1/goals`.
- Three additive fields per goal of `GET /v1/live`: `stepStates`, `review`, `planPending`.
- A proposal, approval, revise and replan on a `done` goal; `goal.reopened`.
- CLI `mesh goal join|leave <G>`, `mesh goal ls --member <user>|--mine`, members in `mesh goal show`.
- The `/healthz` feature `goal-members` and the service scope `goals:join`.

Out:
- **No auto-join on a step claim.** The client's owner chose plain hub membership (D1), not "membership plus
  claim"; taking a step does not make a person a member.
- Notifications or attention items for members; a member role in any rights check (`canApprove` is unchanged).
- Reopening a `dropped` goal: every refusal on `dropped` stays.
- The hub web UI: no change required (it MAY show members later).

## Requirements

- **REQ-GOALMEM-1** `POST /v1/goals/:id/join` MUST record the caller (or the person a service acts for) as a member
  of the goal and answer `200 GoalMembership`. A join by a person who is already a member MUST answer `200` with
  `changed: false` and MUST NOT write a row or emit an event.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-2** `POST /v1/goals/:id/leave` MUST remove the caller's membership and answer `200
  GoalMembership`. A leave by a person who is not a member MUST answer `200` with `changed: false` and MUST NOT
  emit an event.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-3** A join on a `dropped` goal MUST be refused with `409 goal-closed`. A join on any other state,
  `done` included, MUST be accepted. A leave MUST be accepted in every state.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-4** A join that changes the record MUST emit `goal.joined` and a leave that changes it MUST emit
  `goal.left`, each with `user` = the member and, for an act-for request, `via` in the payload (RL-22 rule). Both
  MUST appear in `GET /v1/goals/:id/timeline`.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-5** Both routes MUST accept a service token with the scope `goals:join` acting for a person
  (`X-Mesh-On-Behalf-Of` REQUIRED for a service caller, `onBehalfOfRequired`); the hub MUST honour the person's
  member role only. A person MUST be able to join or leave only for themselves: neither route takes a user in its
  body.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-6** A goal created by a request, `POST /v1/goals` (`createGoal`) and `POST /v1/goals/from-preset`
  (`goalFromPreset`), MUST record the goal owner as a member in the same transaction, emitting `goal.joined` after
  `goal.created`, **unless** the owner is a service user (role `service` without `member`, the rule `live` uses at
  src/hub/service.ts:3867). A goal the hub opens itself through `createWithPlan` from a calibration (src/hub/service.ts:4575),
  a spot check (:6007) or a pulse fire (:7137), and the clean-audit goal (`store.createGoal`, :6617), MUST NOT get
  a member by this rule.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-7** Any other write MUST NOT add or remove a member. A step claim, an offer accept, a plan approval and
  a gate decision MUST NOT change membership.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-8** `GET /v1/goals` MUST carry `members: string[]` (user ids, sorted) on every goal it returns, and
  MUST accept `member=<user id>`, returning only the goals that user is a member of (`member=me`: the caller, or
  the person a service acts for when it sends the header; a service without the header gets its own id, so `[]`).
  An unknown user id MUST return an empty list, not an error. Each goal MUST also carry `planPending` (as in
  REQ-GOALMEM-9). `GET /v1/goals/:id` MUST carry `members` on `GoalDetail`.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-9** Each goal of `GET /v1/live` MUST carry: `stepStates`, an object with one key per `StepState`
  (all nine, zero included) counting the goal's steps in that state, dropped steps included (the unfiltered
  `listSteps({ goal })` result, not the list `live` filters for `done`, `total` and `holders`); `review`, the number
  of findings-review steps in `ready`, `claimed` or `paused`; and `planPending`, true when the goal has a plan with
  `decision: "pending"`, read once per call for every goal (`pendingPlanGoals()`), not one plan read per goal; a
  `dropped` goal MUST report `planPending: false` on `GET /v1/live` and `GET /v1/goals` (its pending plan can no
  longer be decided into work). `done`, `total` and `holders` MUST keep their meaning.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-10** `GET /healthz` MUST list the feature `goal-members` in `features` when REQ-GOALMEM-1 to -9, -12
  and REQ-REOPEN-7 are served. `SERVICE_SCOPES` MUST add `goals:join`.
- **REQ-REOPEN-1** `proposePlan` (`POST /v1/goals/:id/plan`) MUST accept a plan for a `done` goal that is not a
  clean-audit goal (`sink`). The goal MUST stay `done` while the plan is pending. A `dropped` goal MUST still be
  refused.
- **REQ-REOPEN-2** When `approvePlan` approves a plan of a goal whose state was `done` and `goalComplete` is false
  after the materialization, `applyApproved` MUST set the goal to `in_progress` and MUST emit `goal.reopened`
  (`payload: { from: "done", to: "in_progress", version, ...via }`, `user` = the approver) after `plan.approved`.
  When `goalComplete` is still true (every new step skipped by `when`), the goal MUST stay `done` and no
  `goal.reopened` or second `goal.done` MUST be emitted.
- **REQ-REOPEN-3** `revisePlan` MUST accept the pending plan of a `done` goal and MUST leave the goal `done`.
- **REQ-REOPEN-4** `replan` MUST accept a `done` goal that is not a clean-audit goal: the design step reopens as
  today, the goal moves to `design`, and the hub MUST emit `goal.reopened` (`payload: { from: "done", to: "design",
  comment?, ...via }`) after `goal.replan`. A `dropped` goal MUST still be refused with `409 goal-closed`.
- **REQ-REOPEN-5** A reopened goal MUST complete again by the existing rule: when `goalComplete` holds after a step
  finishes, the goal becomes `done` and `goal.done` is emitted again. The trail's `goal.yaml` roll-up MUST use the
  last `goal.done` (unchanged code, src/hub/service.ts:2809).
- **REQ-REOPEN-6** Every other `goal-closed` refusal on a `done` goal (park, stop-run, drop, and the step writes of
  src/hub/service.ts:3406, :3450, :6882) MUST stay as it is.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-12** Migration 15 MUST insert one `goal_member` row `(goal, owner, at = goal.updated_at)` for each
  goal whose `state` is not `done` or `dropped` and that REQ-GOALMEM-6 would give its owner as a member if it were
  created now: no `refs.pulse`, no `calibration`, no `spotCheck`, no `sink`, and an owner whose `user` row lists
  `member` in `roles` (the effective role set `rolesFor` stores, src/core/roles.ts; `roles_extra` is not read). The backfill is a migration, not a person's write: it MUST NOT emit
  `goal.joined`. Re-running the statement MUST add nothing (insert-or-ignore on the primary key).
- **REQ-REOPEN-7** `GET /v1/goals` MUST accept `state=open|done|dropped|all` (absent: every state, as today).
  `open` MUST return every goal whose state is not `done` or `dropped`, **and** every `done` goal with
  `planPending: true` (it waits for a decision that reopens it, client owner D2 and D5); `done` MUST return the
  other `done` goals only, so each goal is in exactly one of `open`, `done`, `dropped`. The goal's `state` field is
  unchanged (`done`).
- **REQ-REOPEN-8** A `done` goal the hub opened, one with `refs.pulse`, `calibration` or `spotCheck`, MUST NOT
  reopen: `proposePlan` and `replan` on it MUST answer `409 goal-closed` and change nothing (client coordinator
  decision 2026-11-27, mesh-ai `reviews/B-264/r1-code.md` finding 4). A clean-audit (`sink`) goal keeps its 0.1.44
  refusal of a plan (REQ-REOPEN-1) and refuses a replan when done with `409 goal-closed`.
- **REQ-GOALMEM-11** The change MUST be backwards compatible: no route, field, event or code is removed, renamed or
  given a new meaning; a client that ignores `members`, `stepStates`, `review`, `planPending` and the three new
  events keeps working.

### Decisions this spec takes

- **The creator is a member (REQ-GOALMEM-6).** Whoever asks for a goal follows it; without the rule the owner's
  own "My goals" misses the goals they just made. A service-owned goal gets no member, because a service account
  never opens "My goals". The owner MAY leave like anyone.
- **Join is allowed on a done goal (REQ-GOALMEM-3).** A person MAY follow finished work that a later review can
  reopen (REQ-REOPEN-2). A dropped goal never changes again, so joining it records nothing useful.
- **A replan reopens to `design`, an approval to `in_progress` (REQ-REOPEN-2, -4).** D2 names the amendment path.
  The replan path reuses the design step so a design session can write the amendment; the goal then runs the
  normal `design → awaiting_approval → in_progress` path and the approval emits no second `goal.reopened`.
- **Backfill without events (REQ-GOALMEM-12).** The client's coordinator decided the backfill (MESH-OQ-SPEC0137-1,
  2026-11-27) so "My goals" is not empty on day one. The rule is REQ-GOALMEM-6's, applied to open goals only, so a
  backfilled goal is one that would get its owner as a member if it were created today. No person wrote it, so no
  `goal.joined` is emitted and the timeline does not claim a join that did not happen.
- **A done goal with a pending amendment is open (REQ-REOPEN-7).** It waits for the decision that reopens it, so
  a manager's open list and its "pending review" count must see it (client coordinator decision, 2026-11-27).

## Interface contracts

### Store (src/store/hub-store.ts)

Migration 15 in `HUB_MIGRATIONS` and `HUB_MIGRATIONS_PG` (the contract test's count becomes 15). SQLite:

```sql
-- RL-28: one row per goal member.
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS goal_member (goal TEXT NOT NULL, user_id TEXT NOT NULL, at INTEGER NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (goal, user_id));
CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS goal_member_user ON goal_member (user_id);
-- RL-28 REQ-GOALMEM-12: the owner of each open person-origin goal; no event.
INSERT OR IGNORE INTO goal_member (goal, user_id, at)
SELECT g.id, json_extract(g.value, '$.ownerId'), g.updated_at
FROM goal g JOIN user u ON u.id = json_extract(g.value, '$.ownerId')
WHERE g.state NOT IN ('done', 'dropped')
  AND json_extract(g.value, '$.refs.pulse') IS NULL
  AND json_extract(g.value, '$.calibration') IS NULL
  AND json_extract(g.value, '$.spotCheck') IS NULL
  AND json_extract(g.value, '$.sink') IS NULL
  AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM json_each(u.roles) WHERE value = 'member');
```

Postgres (`HUB_MIGRATIONS_PG`, `at BIGINT`):

```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS goal_member (goal TEXT NOT NULL, user_id TEXT NOT NULL, at BIGINT NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (goal, user_id));
CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS goal_member_user ON goal_member (user_id);
INSERT INTO goal_member (goal, user_id, at)
SELECT g.id, g.value::jsonb->>'ownerId', g.updated_at
FROM goal g JOIN "user" u ON u.id = g.value::jsonb->>'ownerId'
WHERE g.state NOT IN ('done', 'dropped')
  AND g.value::jsonb #> '{refs,pulse}' IS NULL
  AND NOT (g.value::jsonb ? 'calibration') AND NOT (g.value::jsonb ? 'spotCheck') AND NOT (g.value::jsonb ? 'sink')
  AND u.roles::jsonb ? 'member'
ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING;
```

## Compatibility

Additive only (REQ-GOALMEM-11). A client of 0.1.44 ignores the new fields and events. Two behaviour changes are
visible to an old client: a `done` goal can now take a plan and leave `done`, and a goal created by a person has one
member. A third follows from reopen: a goal can emit `goal.done` more than once (REQ-REOPEN-5), so a consumer that
counts completions per event counts a reopened goal again (the mesh-ai client counts distinct goals, its spec 0137
REQ-OVW-22). None changes a route, a field or a code. The mesh-ai client gates its join, leave and "My goals" on the
`goal-members` feature and reads the three live fields as OPTIONAL (mesh-ai ADR-0025 D30).
