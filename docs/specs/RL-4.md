# Spec RL-4: Debrief

## Status
Draft

Source: `docs/analysis/2026-11-12-pulse-relay-roster.md` §5.7 (Debrief), §5.3 (dispositions, `via`,
person precedence), §5.6 (the debrief stage of a relay), REQ-8, §5.9 (events), §6 and slice S4 of §9;
`docs/analysis/2026-11-12-claude-cli-spike.md` wins where it differs. Builds on RL-1 (headless steps,
artifacts), RL-2 (`docs/specs/RL-2.md`: review records, items, dispositions, `consumes`/`may_set`/`when`,
`skipped`) and RL-3 (pulses), landed as v0.1.15..v0.1.17. Release v0.1.18.

## Problem

RL-2 lets a stage dispose of items only inside its review record, and a stage that is a person is an
ordinary interactive step: anyone can claim it, it can be offered to any machine and auto-launched
there, the person gets no pre-read, and nothing records what the person said or lets them undo a
decision. Design §5.7 asks for a **debrief**: a step only its named person runs, with an interactive
Claude session that goes through the items one at a time and records each decision with the person's
own words, which the person can revert with one key.

## Scope

In:
- `StepPolicy.mode` value `debrief`; field `assignee` (`@owner` or a user id); plan validation and the
  debrief defaults (`consumes`, `assignee`).
- Hub: debrief steps are never offered (assign, goal assign, stick) and are left out of ready lists;
  claim and take-over only by the assignee; pre-read (`POST /v1/steps/:id/read`, `Step.read`,
  `Round.read`); the debrief view (`GET /v1/steps/:id/debrief`) and the caller's list
  (`GET /v1/debriefs`); dispositions one at a time (`POST /v1/steps/:id/dispositions`, `via:
  "debrief-session"`, `answer`); revert (`POST /v1/steps/:id/dispositions/revert`, `via: "ui"`); every
  debrief disposition and revert also as a step note; the debrief's own review record.
- `mesh_context` for a debrief step: the debrief items (with evidence locations and near-matches) and
  the run's report, fenced and untrusted.
- MCP `mesh_dispose` (daemon: redaction, never queued).
- Built-in skill `mesh-debrief`, shipped like the `mesh` skill (`mesh setup`, uninstall, `mesh doctor`,
  `mesh skill --debrief`, `skills/mesh-debrief/SKILL.md` generated by `pnpm schemas`).
- CLI `mesh debrief <step>` (claims and opens the interactive session in tmux through `mesh run`).
- Daemon local routes for the TUI: `GET /local/debriefs`, `POST /local/revert`.
- TUI `o` panel: debriefs and "decided in debrief" with one-key revert (`u`).
- Web: Mine page **Assigned to you** lists the caller's debriefs and decided items (Revert button);
  the step panel of a debrief step shows the pre-read (report, items), a read button, and the decided
  items with Revert.
- Docs: concepts, how-to, the mesh skill, the plan API table, the events list.

Out:
- A debrief stage in the built-in `audit-relay` preset (stays agent-only; Open questions).
- Missed pulse slots in the TUI (RL-3 deferred it here; Open questions).
- Re-evaluating skipped or ready steps after a revert or a debrief disposition (RL-2 re-evaluation
  scope stands).
- Calibration counting and confirmation of `via: debrief-session` dispositions (S6; design OQ-2
  default: the revert is enough).
- OpenCode debrief sessions; `mesh debrief --bg`.
- Debriefs in `mesh offers` / `mesh_offers` output.

## Interface contracts

Names used below (RL-2's names stand: **run** of a consuming step, **run's record**, **lineage**,
**current round** = number of the step's rounds + 1, severity order, **items a step may act on now** =
items of its run's record whose row state is in the step's `consumes`). **Debrief step** = a step with
`policy.mode === "debrief"`. **Assignee** of a debrief step = `policy.assignee`, where `"@owner"` (and an
absent value) resolves to the goal's `ownerId` at the time of the check. **Debrief items** of a debrief
step = the items it may act on now, ordered most severe first, then `first.at`, then `fp` (as
`GET /v1/items`). **Debrief disposition** = a disposition history entry with `via: "debrief-session"`.
**Revertable**: a debrief disposition is revertable when it is the latest `item_history` entry (any
kind) of its `(lineage, fp)`. **Note text** of a disposition: `debrief: <disposition> <fp> "<title>":
<reason>`; of a revert: `debrief: reverted <fp> to <to> (was <from>)`. `<title>` is the item row's title.

### Hub (src/hub/service.ts, src/hub/app.ts, listed in `HUB_API`)

**Never offered.** `POST /v1/steps/:id/assign` on a debrief step → 409 `<id> is a debrief step: only
<assignee> runs it (mesh debrief <id>)` (after the existing gate and state checks). Goal assign
(`POST /v1/goals/:id/assign`) and a goal's stick (`emitReady`) skip debrief steps (not offered, not
counted; goal assign with only debrief steps ready → the existing 409 `<goal> has no ready step to
offer`). `GET /v1/steps?ready=1` leaves debrief steps out (so `mesh next`, `mesh_next`,
`mesh_context` without a step, the TUI `n` key and autopilot chaining never pick one). No offer message
is ever queued for a debrief step.

**Claim.** `POST /v1/steps/:id/claim` (and `mesh_claim`, `mesh run --step`) and
`POST /v1/steps/:id/take` on a debrief step by anyone but its assignee → 403 `<id> is a debrief for
<assignee>; only they can take it` (also for admins and the goal owner), checked before the claim rules.

**Done.** RL-2's REQ-5 rule applies (a debrief step has `output`). The round gets `read: {user, at}`
when `Step.read.round` equals the round's `n`.

| Route | Rule, scope, tag | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /v1/steps/:id/read` (body `{}`) | member, `steps` | 404 `no step <id>`; not a debrief → 409 `<id> is not a debrief step`; caller not the assignee → 403 `<id> is a debrief for <assignee>; only they can take it`; state not `ready`, `claimed` or `paused` → 409 `<id> is <state>`. Sets `Step.read = {user: caller, at: now, round: current round}` (a later read replaces it). 200 `{read}`. Event `step.read {round}`. |
| `GET /v1/steps/:id/debrief` | member, `goals:read`, `steps` | 404 `no step <id>`; not a debrief → 409 `<id> is not a debrief step`. 200 `DebriefView` (any member may read it). Without a run or run's record: `items: []`, no `run`/`lineage` when there is no run. |
| `GET /v1/debriefs` | member, `steps` | `DebriefList` for the caller: `debriefs` = debrief steps in state `ready`, `claimed` or `paused` whose assignee is the caller, by step id (`cmp`); `decided` = debrief dispositions whose `decider.user` is the caller, `at` within the last 7 days (`at ≥ now − 7 d`) and revertable, newest first. |
| `POST /v1/steps/:id/dispositions` (`DisposeRequest`) | member, `steps`, 201 | In order: 404 `no step <id>`; not a debrief → 409 `<id> is not a debrief step; a stage records its dispositions in its review record (mesh_review)`; state not `claimed`/`paused` → 409 `<id> is <state>; only a held debrief takes dispositions`; caller not `holderUser` → 403 `<id> is held by <holderUser>`; `sid` of another user's session → 403 `session <sid> is not yours on this machine`; a review record exists for (step, current round) → 409 `<id> round <n> already has its review record`; then the RL-2 disposition checks 1, 2, 4 and 6 (texts exactly as RL-2, without the `disposition of <fp>: ` prefix) → 422 with that text; check 6 passes also when the item's latest disposition entry is this step's in the current round (the person changes a decision). Applied as RL-2 "Applying a disposition" with decider `{kind: "person", user: caller}`, `via: "debrief-session"`, `answer`, `round` = current round; adds a note (note text, as `POST /v1/steps/:id/note` does, event `step.noted`); event `item.disposed` (`via: "debrief-session"`). 201 `DisposeResult`. |
| `POST /v1/steps/:id/dispositions/revert` (`RevertRequest`) | member, `steps` | In order: 404 `no step <id>`; not a debrief → 409 `<id> is not a debrief step`; no debrief disposition of the fp by this step → 404 `<id> has no debrief disposition of <fp>`; caller is not that disposition's `decider.user`, the goal owner or an admin → 403 `only <decider user>, the goal owner or an admin can revert this`; the latest such disposition is not revertable → 409 `<fp> changed after the debrief decided it; nothing to revert`. Effect on the item row: `state` := the disposition's `from`; `dismissedEh` := the row's `eh` when the restored state is `dismissed`, else removed; `until` removed unless the restored state is `deferred`; `link` unchanged. History entry `kind: "revert"` (`from` = the disposition, `to` = restored state, decider the caller, `via: "ui"`, `reason`, `goal`/`step` = the debrief step, `round` = the disposition's round). A note (revert note text). Event `item.reverted`. Works in any step state. 200 `RevertResult`. |

**The debrief's own review record** (`POST /v1/steps/:id/review` on a debrief step; RL-2 rules
otherwise): its decider is `{kind: "person", user: caller}`; each disposition it applies is stored with
`via: "debrief-session"` (no `answer`) and adds a note; a disposition whose fp's latest disposition
entry is this step's in the current round with the **same** disposition, and whose item state still
equals it, is not applied again and not a problem: its fp is listed in `ReviewResult.already` (checked
after RL-2 check 4, before check 6). A differing disposition goes through check 6 as usual. A debrief
record with `dispositions: []` is valid. `review.recorded` gains `already: number`.

**`GET /v1/steps/:id/context`** of a debrief step: `inputs` start with (before the policy's `inputs`,
under RL-1's caps) `{from: <run id>, artifact: "debrief", kind: "review", title: "debrief items (<n>)",
content: JSON.stringify({lineage, items: DebriefItem[]}), truncated}` when there are debrief items, then
`{from: <run id>, artifact: <report id>, kind: "report", title: <artifact title or "report">, content,
truncated}` when `DebriefView.report` has content.

**Events** (`docs/events.md`; payloads never carry titles, reasons, answers, evidence or report text):
- `item.disposed`: `via: "stage" | "debrief-session"` (payload keys as RL-2).
- `item.reverted` (user, goal, step): `lineage, fp, from, to: string`, `round: number`, `via: "ui"`.
- `step.read` (user, goal, step): `round: number`.
- `review.recorded`: gains `already: number`.

- **MCP `mesh_dispose`**: session holds no step → `this session holds no step`. Redacts `reason` and

  with `sid` and the shim's key as `Idempotency-Key`. Reply: `Recorded <fp>: <disposition> (was

  the hub's text. Hub offline → tool error `the hub is offline; the disposition was not recorded.
  <offline tail>` (never queued; the link is checked at once first, as `mesh_review`).

- **`brief()`** of a debrief step adds, after the Brief block, the line `This step is a debrief with

### TUI (src/cli/tui/tui.ts)

The `o` panel loads `client.offers()` and `client.debriefs()` (every 5 s, as offers) and renders with
`renderOffers`; the cursor moves over offers, then debriefs, then decided entries. `⏎` on an offer
accepts (as today); `⏎` on a debrief sets status `Start it with: mesh debrief <step>`; `u` on a
decided entry calls `client.revert(step, fp)` and sets status `Reverted <fp> to <state>.` (the restored
state) or the error text, then reloads; `d` acts only on offers. The sessions view's offers badge counts
offers + debriefs.

## Acceptance criteria

Surfaces: unit tests (`src/core`, `src/protocol`, `src/cli/tui/render.ts`); World tests
(`test/helpers/world.ts`, `review-kit.ts`, `relay-kit.ts`; the test side may extend them); CLI tests
with a fake `runner` (tmux and `claude` are never run; no test runs the real `claude` binary);
Playwright (`test/e2e-web`, `pnpm test:web`). Fixture rules: a daemon reports only repos with a git
remote, and only when its `shareSessions` is `me` or `all`; tests that need the daemon to know a repo or
a session without a step set both. World tests that move the clock past 14 days give device tokens a

## Open questions

- 2026-11-12 **Defaults of a debrief step.** Design §5.3 gives the debrief default `consumes: [open,
  confirmed]`; RL-2 left it to S4. Taken: `policyOf` fills it and `assignee: "@owner"` when absent, and
  `validatePolicies` checks as if they were set. `output: review-record` and `may_set` stay explicit
  (errors), since the design's example names them and a debrief that can set nothing is a plan mistake.
- 2026-11-12 **Assignee resolution and membership.** `@owner` is resolved at each check (claim, read,
  lists), so an ownership change moves the debrief. A named assignee is not checked for hub membership
  at plan time; an unknown user simply never sees it (owner/admin can replan). Admins and the owner
  cannot claim a debrief assigned to someone else (REQ-8 "claimable only by its assignee"); they can
  replan it or revert its decisions.
- 2026-11-12 **Ready lists.** "Never offered and never auto-launched" is read as: no offer path (assign,
  goal assign, stick, pulses) offers a debrief, and `GET /v1/steps?ready=1` omits debriefs, so
  `mesh_next`, `mesh next`, the TUI `n` key and autopilot chaining never pick one. The assignee finds it
  under Assigned to you (web, TUI `o`). `mesh offers`/`mesh_offers` do not list debriefs (Out).
- 2026-11-12 **Where `mesh_dispose` applies.** Only on a held debrief step; other consuming stages keep
  recording dispositions in their review record (RL-2). A person may change a decision within the same
  round through `mesh_dispose` (the state check accepts the step's own latest disposition of that
  round). `mesh_dispose` is never queued offline (a disposition is a decision the person watches).
- 2026-11-12 **The debrief's own record vs `mesh_dispose`.** Design §5.7 asks for both. Taken: the
  record's dispositions that repeat a `mesh_dispose` of the same round (same disposition, state
  unchanged) are reported in `already` and not applied twice; any other disposition in it is applied
  (with `via: "debrief-session"`, no `answer`) or refused by the RL-2 checks. `ReviewResult.already` is
  new for this.
- 2026-11-12 **Revert shape.** `via: ui` is recorded as a history entry `kind: "revert"` restoring the
  disposition's `from` state, not as a new disposition (the restored state may be `open`, which is not
  a `Disposition`). Only the latest history entry of an item is revertable, so a revert never undoes a
  later fold or decision. Who may revert: the deciding person, the goal owner or an admin. Design OQ-2
  default stands: the revert is the confirmation; nothing else gates `debrief-session` decisions.
- 2026-11-12 **No re-evaluation after a revert.** A revert after the debrief is done does not reopen or
  re-settle later stages (RL-2's re-evaluation scope); a person replans or reopens if needed.
- 2026-11-12 **Pre-read storage.** Design §6 names `Round.read`; a round exists only after done, so the
  read is kept on the step (`Step.read`, last read wins) and copied into the round at done. The read is
  recorded only by an explicit action (web button), never by opening the panel.
- 2026-11-12 **Evidence and near-matches.** The view and the `debrief` input carry evidence locations
  (path, lines) from the run's record without the agent's `note`; the skill reads the lines from the
  repo (design: "rendered from the repo, not the agent's quote"). Near-match = same rule and path,
  other fingerprint (design §5.3), over all item rows of the lineage.
- 2026-11-12 **Skill activation.** The spike does not cover skills in interactive sessions; the
  debrief prompt names the `mesh-debrief` skill and the brief repeats it, relying on Claude Code's
  description-based skill loading. Revisit if sessions do not load it.
- 2026-11-12 **`audit-relay` stays agent-only.** RL-3 said S4 "adds the debrief step"; the brief for
  this row does not, and adding it would make every existing pulse of `audit-relay` wait for its owner.
  A repo can add a debrief stage in its own `.mesh/presets` (snapshotted per R-6). A built-in variant
  with a debrief is left for a later row.
- 2026-11-12 **Missed pulse slots in the TUI.** RL-3 asked S4 to carry a criterion for REQ-2's TUI
  view; the brief for this row does not include it. Left for a later row; `mesh pulse ls`/`show` and
  the web Mine page show them.
- 2026-11-12 **"Decided in debrief" window.** The Mine/TUI list shows the caller's revertable debrief
  decisions of the last 7 days; older ones stay revertable through the step panel and the route.
