## Problem

1. **Nothing stops a headless run on request.** The daemon kills a headless child only on its
   budget (src/daemon/runs.ts:766-773) or after the grace period that follows its `mesh_done`
   (runs.ts:750-762). A park (RL-22) stops nothing: the run keeps working until it ends, using its
   turns on a step it no longer holds.
2. **Nothing stops an interactive session remotely, and the owner allows it only with consent.** A
   stalled interactive session is nudged twice, then only a local notice fires
   (src/daemon/daemon.ts:1492). Typing into a person's terminal from another person's request is a
   new reach onto their machine; the owner allowed it only per machine, opt-in, default off.
3. **An act-for assign reaches only the person's own machines.** `resolveTarget` with `owner`
   (src/hub/service.ts:639-665) refuses every machine of another user (`403 machine-not-yours`,
   RL-21). A dashboard cannot route a step to a teammate machine, and nothing tells it which
   teammates are willing to receive offers.
4. **A listed headless offer cannot be accepted.** A headless offer runs only when its target's
   accept policy auto-accepts it (daemon.ts:852-853); `mesh accept` refuses a headless step
   (daemon.ts:996-997, RL-1 open question "manual accept"). An offer whose consent is a person's
   explicit accept therefore can never run headless.

## Scope

In:
- `POST /v1/steps/:id/stop-run`: the hub parks the step at once and asks the run's machine to kill
  the run; the daemon kills it (as on budget), reports exit `killed_operator`, uploads the
  transcript tail and keeps any commits in the run's worktree.
- `POST /v1/sessions/:sid/stop` with `mode: "interrupt" | "end"`: only on a machine whose daemon
  config `remoteStop` is `true` (default `false`); the hub refuses otherwise and the daemon
  re-checks its own config.
- A new service scope `sessions:stop`; act-for on both stop routes with the person's `member` role
  only (never the `approver` or the `admin` role).
- Daemon config `teamOffers` (default `false`): the machine's owner opts in to offers made for
  teammates through a service. Machine facts `remoteStop`, `teamOffers`, `runStop` on sync.
- `GET /v1/machines?offers=team`: the teammate machines that accept team offers.
- An act-for `POST /v1/steps/:id/assign` MAY target a teammate machine that opted in, never a
  teammate's session; such an offer is never auto-accepted.
- Manual accept of a listed headless offer: `mesh accept <step>` on the target machine starts the
  headless run.
- `OfferView` and the daemon's offer notice show the mode, the profile, `via` and `team`.
- Events `run.stop_requested`, `run.stopped`, `session.stop_requested`, `session.stopped`,
  `machine.consent_changed`; `step.parked` and `step.offered` gain fields.
- CLI `mesh stop`, `mesh session stop`, `mesh setup --remote-stop`, `mesh setup --team-offers`.
- `/healthz` features `run-stop`, `session-stop`, `team-offers`.

Out:
- Fleet pause and alert delivery (RL-24 and RL-25, mesh-ai rows B-250 and B-251).
- Honouring a person's `approver` or `admin` role through act-for on any route of this spec
  (MESH-OQ-AIOPS-14, owned by the mesh-ai owner, is the route by which the approver role could be
  added later, after an ADR-0025 amendment).
- Stopping an OpenCode session; stopping a session on a machine that has not opted in, by anyone,
  through any route (the machine owner stops their own sessions locally).
- A pool or shared-runner machine kind, and a team accept rule in `AcceptPolicy`: consent is the
  machine owner's per-offer accept (§Team offers).
- Any change to an own-token assign (MM-3): a member's own `mesh assign` to a teammate machine
  keeps today's behaviour, accept rules included (Open question 1).
- A migration: steps, offers and machine facts are JSON values (schema stays 12).

## Terms

- *Teammate*: another non-service user of the same hub as the person (the caller, or the person a
  service acts for).
- *Teammate machine*: a machine whose owner is a teammate.
- *Team offer*: an offer made through act-for to a teammate machine (§Team offers).

## Requirements

- **REQ-RUNSTOP-1** `POST /v1/steps/:id/stop-run` on a run-held step (§Stop a run) MUST, in one
  transaction, park the step with `parkedReason = "stopped: <reason>"`, record `stoppedRun` on the
  step, queue exactly one inbox message (machine-wide, kind `run_stop`, to the holder machine), and
  emit `run.stop_requested` and `step.parked`. It MUST NOT queue RL-22's `parked` session message.
- **REQ-RUNSTOP-2** A daemon that receives a `run_stop` message for a running run of that step MUST
  end the run's process as it ends a run over budget (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL), MUST report the run's
  exit as `killed_operator`, MUST NOT call `POST /v1/steps/:id/done` or push for that run, and MUST
  upload one `transcript` artifact of the run's tail.
- **REQ-RUNSTOP-3** A queued run of that step MUST be dropped from the queue without starting.
- **REQ-RUNSTOP-4** The hub MUST refuse `stop-run` with `409 run-stop-unsupported` and change
  nothing when the holder machine has not reported `runStop: true`.
- **REQ-SESSSTOP-1** `POST /v1/sessions/:sid/stop` MUST be refused with `409 stop-refused`, change
  nothing and queue no message, unless the session's machine last reported `remoteStop: true`.
- **REQ-SESSSTOP-2** A daemon whose own config has `remoteStop` not `true` MUST NOT act on a `stop`
  message, whatever the hub stored, and MUST ack it `failed` with method `stop-refused`.
- **REQ-SESSSTOP-3** A daemon that acts on a `stop` message MUST notify the machine's owner through
  its configured notify channels, naming the person who stopped the session, `via` when present,
  the mode and the reason.
- **REQ-SESSSTOP-4** `daemon.json` without `remoteStop` MUST behave as `remoteStop: false`.
- **REQ-STOPRIGHTS-1** A request under act-for on either stop route MUST run with the person's
  `member` role only; it MUST NOT run with the person's `approver` or `admin` role (mesh-ai
  ADR-0025 D23.2; RL-22 REQ-DECIDEFOR-3).
- **REQ-STOPRIGHTS-2** The machine-owner right of `stop-run` (§Stop a run) MUST apply only to a
  request made with the person's own token; a request under act-for MUST NOT use it.
- **REQ-TEAMOFFER-1** An act-for assign MAY target a teammate machine only when that machine last
  reported `teamOffers: true`; any other teammate machine MUST be refused `403 machine-not-yours`.
- **REQ-TEAMOFFER-2** An act-for assign MUST NOT target a session on a teammate machine.
- **REQ-TEAMOFFER-3** A team offer MUST be stored and sent with `auto: false`, whatever the target's
  accept policy says, so nothing runs before the machine's owner accepts it.
- **REQ-TEAMOFFER-4** `GET /v1/machines?offers=team` MUST list exactly the teammate machines that
  last reported `teamOffers: true`, and MUST NOT list the person's own machines or a service
  account's machine.
- **REQ-TEAMOFFER-5** `daemon.json` without `teamOffers` MUST behave as `teamOffers: false`.
- **REQ-HLACCEPT-1** `mesh accept <step>` on the machine a listed headless offer targets MUST start
  the headless run of that offer (the same path as an auto-accepted headless offer), or refuse with
  a message; it MUST NOT bind the step to an interactive session.
- **REQ-HLACCEPT-2** The daemon MUST keep a listed headless offer message until that offer is no
  longer `offered` to this machine (or its run started from it), whatever the inbox lists, and
  across a daemon restart.
- **REQ-COMPAT-1** No `error` text of a refusal that 0.1.41 returns MUST change; `code` is added
  beside it. A client that reads only `error` MUST keep working.
- **REQ-COMPAT-2** The hub MUST NOT queue a `run_stop` or `stop` message to a machine whose last
  sync came from a daemon older than this release, including a daemon downgraded after it reported
  `runStop` or `remoteStop` (the hub refuses first, REQ-RUNSTOP-4, REQ-SESSSTOP-1; §Daemon
  behaviour, sync facts).

## Interface contracts

### Scopes (src/protocol/hub-api.ts)

`SERVICE_SCOPES` gains `"sessions:stop"`, appended after the scopes RL-22 and RL-24 add.

Why a new scope for session stop: interrupting a person's live terminal is a different reach from
deciding a goal (`goals:decide`) or writing into a session (`sessions:message`); one scope per kind
of write is the existing pattern. `stop-run` uses `goals:decide` (as the client's ADR-0025 D23.1
names it): a headless run executes under a pinned profile with no person at the keyboard, and
stopping it is a decision about the step, like park. An existing token MUST be re-minted to gain
`sessions:stop`.

### Routes

| Route | rule | scope | act-for | header for a service | roles kept under act-for | hub check (against the person) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /v1/steps/:id/stop-run` (new) | member | `goals:decide` | yes | required | none (member only) | §Stop a run |
| `POST /v1/sessions/:sid/stop` (new) | member | `sessions:stop` | yes | required | none (member only) | §Stop a session |
| `GET /v1/machines` (new; `offers=team` required) | member | `live:read` | yes | required (`onBehalfOfRequired`) | none | §Team machines |
| `POST /v1/steps/:id/assign` (exists) | member | `steps:assign` | yes | required | none (RL-21) | RL-21 §Rights, with §Team offers |

Neither stop route sets RL-24's `onBehalfOfRoles`: under act-for `actingFor` returns
`roles: ["member"]` (unchanged), so `canApprove` holds for the goal owner only, as on RL-22's routes
(mesh-ai ADR-0025 D23.1, D23.2; RL-22 REQ-DECIDEFOR-3). An approver who does not own the goal stops
a run or a session with their own login. MESH-OQ-AIOPS-14 (RL-22 open question 1) is the only route
by which this could widen. The OpenAPI generator documents the
header on each; `node scripts/schemas.ts` regenerates `schemas/hub-api.openapi.json`. Every write
takes `Idempotency-Key` (keys per `<service>><person>`, unchanged).

### Stop a run: `POST /v1/steps/:id/stop-run`

Body `StopRun = z.object({ reason: z.string().trim().min(1).max(500) }).strict()`.

*Run-held for a stop* (`stoppable`): the step is `claimed` or `paused`, has `holderMachine`, and
either RL-21's `runHeld(step)` is true, or `step.holderSid` is absent and `step.offer` is `accepted`
with `mode: "headless"` and `offer.to.machine === step.holderMachine` (a run accepted but not yet
started or not yet bound to its session).

Rights: `canApprove(actor, goal.ownerId)` (with the person's own token: owner, approver or admin;
under act-for: the goal owner only, REQ-STOPRIGHTS-1), or, with the person's own token only
(`actor.via` absent, REQ-STOPRIGHTS-2), the actor owns the holder machine
(`machine.userId === actor.userId`: a person may stop a run on their own machine from their own
login). ADR-0025 D23.1's hub check for this row is `canApprove`; the machine-owner branch is a
person's own right on their own machine and is never relayed. Checked in this order, inside
`goalTx`:

1. Step unknown → `404 step-unknown`; goal `done` or `dropped` → `409 goal-closed`.
2. Not allowed → `403 not-approver`.
3. Not `stoppable` → `409 not-a-run` (`<id> is not held by a headless run; park it instead (mesh step park <id>)`).
4. Holder machine without `runStop: true` → `409 run-stop-unsupported`
   (`<machine name> runs an older mesh that cannot stop a run; park the step and ask <owner> to upgrade`).

Effect:
- The park effect of RL-22 REQ-PARK-1 (its routes row `POST /v1/steps/:id/park` and §Park: state
  `parked`, `parkedAt`, holder fields removed, no round, no `outcome`, an open offer withdrawn),
  with `parkedReason: "stopped: <reason>"`. RL-22's `parked` message to the holder session is NOT
  queued: the run that holds the step is being killed and would never read it, so the stop queues
  exactly one message, the `run_stop` below.
- `step.stoppedRun = { machine, holder, at, by, runId?, via? }`: `machine` and `holder` are the
  previous `holderMachine` and `holderUser`; `runId` the `runId` of the step's latest `run.started`
  event, when one exists. `Step` gains `stoppedRun` as an OPTIONAL field; `POST /v1/steps/:id/resume`
  removes it.
- One machine-wide message (`toSid: ""`) of kind `run_stop` to that machine:
  text `MESH: @<person> stopped the headless run of <step>: <reason>` (plus ` (via <service>)`),
  data `{ step, goal, reason, by, runId?, via? }`.
- Events: `run.stop_requested` (`user` = the actor; payload `{ machine, runId?, reason, via? }`),
  then `step.parked` (payload as RL-22 plus `stopped: true`).

Reply: `200 { step: Step, message: number, online: boolean }` (`message`: the `run_stop` message id;
`online`: the machine was seen within the online window).

While `stoppedRun` is set and the step is `parked`, `POST /v1/steps/:id/artifacts` accepts **one**
artifact of kind `transcript` from a device token whose `machineId === stoppedRun.machine` and whose
user is `stoppedRun.holder`, stored at round `rounds.length + 1` under the existing size and
per-round limits; every other upload keeps today's `409` (`only a held step takes artifacts`).

`RunExit` (src/protocol/goal.ts) admits `killed_operator`. `run.finished` and `GET /v1/runs` show it
as any exit; `overBudget` stays `exit === "killed_budget"`.

### Stop a session: `POST /v1/sessions/:sid/stop`

Body `StopSession = z.object({ mode: z.enum(["interrupt", "end"]), reason: z.string().trim().min(1).max(500) }).strict()`.

- `interrupt`: the agent stops its current turn; the session stays open at its prompt. Only a
  session in a tmux pane can be interrupted; a background session acks `failed` `no-pane`
  (§Daemon behaviour).
- `end`: interrupt when the session has a pane, then the session's agent process ends.

Checked in this order:

1. No session `:sid` (exact sid) → `404 session-unknown`; session ended → `409 session-ended`.
2. A headless run's session (`kind: "print"`) → `409 session-is-run`
   (`<sid> is a headless run; stop it with mesh stop <step>`).
3. Rights: the session's owner (`hs.userId === actor.userId`), or `canApprove` on the goal of the
   step the session holds (`session.step`), or an admin with their own token. Otherwise
   `403 not-allowed`. Under act-for the person is a member only (REQ-STOPRIGHTS-1): `canApprove`
   holds for the goal owner only, and the admin branch is not reachable.
4. The session's machine has not reported `remoteStop: true` → `409 stop-refused`
   (`<machine name>'s owner has not allowed remote stops; ask <owner> to stop it`).
5. The session's agent is OpenCode → `409 stop-unsupported`.

Effect: one machine-wide message of kind `stop` to the session's machine, data
`{ sid, mode, reason, by, via? }`, text `MESH: @<person> asks to <interrupt|end> session <name>: <reason>`
(plus ` (via <service>)`); event `session.stop_requested` (`user` = the actor, `sid`, payload
`{ machine, mode, reason, step?, via? }`). The step the session holds is not changed; a caller that
wants it parked calls RL-22 park.

Reply: `200 { message: number, online: boolean }`.

### Message acks of `run_stop` and `stop`

`ack` (service.ts:3380) reads the acked message (`store.getMessage`). Only when `ackMessage`
returns `true` (the first ack of a `pending` message, as `message.delivered` today) it also emits,
for kind `run_stop`, `run.stopped`, and for kind `stop`, `session.stopped`; a retried ack emits
neither. `user` = the acking machine's user; payload
`{ machine, outcome: <ack method>, state: <ack state>, by, via?, step?, runId?, sid?, mode? }`.
Outcomes (the ack `method`): `run_stop` → `killed`, `dropped` (queued, never started),
`already-ended` (the run's process had ended before the stop; it finishes as it would have),
`no-run` (nothing of that step runs here); `stop` → `interrupted`, `ended`, `stop-refused`,
`no-pane`, `no-session`. `message.delivered` is emitted as today.

### Daemon behaviour

Config (`DaemonConfig`, src/protocol/config.ts), both local and reported to the hub:
- `remoteStop: z.boolean().default(false)` — the owner allows `stop` messages to act on this machine.
- `teamOffers: z.boolean().default(false)` — the owner allows offers a service makes for a teammate.

`SyncRequest.machine` gains `remoteStop?`, `teamOffers?` and `runStop?` (`true` when the daemon has
this release's `run_stop` handling and a headless runner). A daemon of this release sends all three
on every sync that carries `machine`. When a sync carries `machine`, the hub stores each of the three
as sent and stores an absent one as `false`, so a sync from a downgraded daemon clears them
(REQ-COMPAT-2); a sync without `machine` keeps the stored values.
`MachineRow`/`MachineFacts` gain the three fields (stored in the machine's JSON extra, as `accept`).
When a sync changes `remoteStop` or `teamOffers` from its stored value (absent counts as `false`),
the hub emits `machine.consent_changed` (`user` = the machine's owner, payload
`{ machine, remoteStop, teamOffers }`).

`receive` (daemon.ts:761) handles machine-wide kinds `offer` (today), `run_stop` and `stop`; any
other kind stays `unknown` as today. Both new kinds are acked with `Idempotency`-safe handling as
offers are (`offersHandled`): a retried pull never stops twice.

`run_stop` (`HeadlessRuns.stop(step, runId?)`):
- A run of `step` (and of `runId` when given) in state `running`: save `stoppedByOperator: true`
  and `stopReason` on the run file, then `runner.kill(pid, startId)` as `checkBudget` does. Ack
  `delivered`, method `killed`.
- In state `queued`: mark it `released` without starting it. Ack `delivered`, method `dropped`.
- In state `running` with `endedAt` set (the process ended; `finish` has not completed, for example
  while the hub is away): nothing is killed, `stoppedByOperator` is NOT set, and the run finishes
  with the exit it had (a run that ended on its own never reports `killed_operator`). Ack
  `delivered`, method `already-ended`.
- None: ack `delivered`, method `no-run`.
- The machine owner is notified: `@<by> stopped the headless run of <step>: <reason>`.

End of a stopped run (`finish`, runs.ts:844): `telemetry` gives exit `killed_operator` when
`stoppedByOperator` (before the `killed_budget` and `no_result` cases, core/telemetry.ts:152). The
step decision is skipped: upload the transcript tail (title `run transcript (killed_operator)`), no
`/done`, no push, then the `finished` run report without `round`. A `4xx` answer to any of these
calls ends that call (no retry), as today. The run's sessions end as today. The worktree is removed
only when its `HEAD` equals `baseCommit`; otherwise it is kept and its path is logged and named in
the owner notice (`commits kept in <path>`).

`stop` (`Daemon.stopSession`):
- `remoteStop` not `true` in the daemon's own config → ack `failed`, method `stop-refused`
  (REQ-SESSSTOP-2), no notice beyond the log.
- No live local session `sid` → ack `failed`, `no-session`.
- `mode: interrupt`, a session in a tmux pane → `tmux send-keys -t <pane> Escape` (the agent's
  interrupt key). Ack `delivered`, `interrupted`.
- `mode: interrupt`, a background session (`bgId`, no pane) → nothing is sent; ack `failed`,
  `no-pane`. A background session has no interrupt key, and `claude stop <bgId>` stops the
  session's process (src/claude/background.ts:20), which is `end`, not `interrupt`.
- `mode: interrupt`, neither a pane nor a `bgId` → ack `failed`, `no-pane`.
- `mode: end`, a session in a tmux pane → the interrupt above, then SIGTERM to the session's `pid`
  (the agent process, never the pane's shell); no `pid` → ack `failed`, `no-pane`.
- `mode: end`, a background session → `claude stop <bgId>`.
- `mode: end`, neither → ack `failed`, `no-pane`.
- After an `end` that acted, the session is marked ended with
  `endedReason: "stopped by @<by>: <reason>"`. Ack `delivered`, `ended`.
- Every acted-on stop notifies the machine owner (REQ-SESSSTOP-3):
  `@<by> <interrupted|ended> <session name>: <reason>` (plus ` (via <service>)`).

### Team machines: `GET /v1/machines?offers=team`

Query `offers` is REQUIRED and MUST equal `team` (else `400 bad-query`). Reply
`{ machines: TeamMachine[] }`, sorted online first, then by owner, then by name:

`TeamMachine = { id, name, nickname?, owner, labels, online, lastSeen, headless, repos }`
(`repos`: repo names; `headless`: the machine reported it can run headless steps).

Members: the teammate machines (§Terms) of the caller (or of the acted-for person) with
`teamOffers: true`; a service account's machine is never listed; the person's own machines are never
listed. A
`live:read` service MUST send the header (`400 on-behalf-of-required` otherwise).

### Team offers (act-for `POST /v1/steps/:id/assign`)

RL-21 §Rights step 3 changes for act-for only. After the machine is resolved:

| Resolved machine | Session part in `to` | Result |
|---|---|---|
| the person's own | any | RL-21 unchanged |
| a teammate machine, `teamOffers` not `true` | any | `403 machine-not-yours` (RL-21 unchanged) |
| a teammate machine, `teamOffers: true` | none | allowed: a team offer |
| a teammate machine, `teamOffers: true` | present | `403 team-offer-session` (`a teammate's machine takes the offer, never one of their sessions: name <machine> only`) |

For a team offer:
- `mode`/`profile` in the body → `403 team-offer-override`, with RL-21's unchanged `error` text
  (`machine not yours: <to>`, REQ-COMPAT-1): a teammate machine receives only what the step
  declares. The distinct `code` lets a client tell "not opted in" (`machine-not-yours`) from "opted
  in, no launch-time override" (`team-offer-override`), one code per case (ADR-0025 D23.7). A declared
  headless step goes through `headlessGuard` as today (`headless-unsupported` and the profile codes).
- The offer is stored and messaged with `auto: false` (REQ-TEAMOFFER-3) and `team: true`;
  `StepOffer` gains `team?: true`.
- `step.offered` payload gains `team: true`. The offer message text keeps its prefix (the daemon
  parses it) and ends ` (via <service>, team offer)`.
- The machine's owner sees it in `GET /v1/offers` and accepts (`mesh accept <step>`) or declines
  (`mesh decline <step>`) with their own login, as any MM-3 offer. Accept makes the owner the step's
  holder. A team offer that nobody accepts expires as any offer (`offerExpiryMs`, 30 minutes by
  default, src/hub/config.ts); an expiry is the normal end of an unanswered team offer, not a
  fault.

`OfferView` gains `mode?: "headless"`, `profile?: { name, sha256 }` and `team?: true`. The daemon's
listed-offer notice (daemon.ts:837-840) becomes
`@<by> offers <step> "<title>" (repo <repo>[, headless, profile <name>][, via <service>]): mesh accept <step> or mesh decline <step>`,
and `mesh offers` shows the same fields on each line.

### Manual accept of a headless offer

A listed headless offer is acked `delivered`/`listed` at once (daemon.ts:822-846), and the hub's
inbox lists only `pending` messages (hub-store.ts:1510), so the message is gone from the next pull.
Retention therefore follows the offer, not the inbox (REQ-HLACCEPT-2):

- When `handleOffer` lists a headless offer that is not `auto`, the daemon first writes the message
  to the daemon store's `kv` table (src/store/daemon-store.ts) under the key
  `headless-offer:<step>`, value `{ message, keptAt }`, then acks it. A later offer message for the
  same step replaces the value. The `kv` table is the daemon's SQLite file, so the entry survives a
  daemon restart.
- The entry is removed when: (a) an accept started or queued the run from it; (b) the step read an
  accept makes (`GET /v1/steps/:id`) shows `step.offer` not `offered` to this machine; (c) a sweep
  finds the step absent from `GET /v1/offers` for this machine. The sweep runs on daemon start and
  then once an hour while the hub is reachable. No other event (an inbox pull, an ack, a message
  no longer listed) removes it.

`POST /local/accept` (daemon.ts:988)
for a step whose open offer targets this machine with `mode: "headless"` (or whose step declares
`policy.mode: "headless"`):
- with `target`/`--session` → `409` (`<step> runs headless; mesh accept <step> without --session`);
- no headless runner on this machine → `409` (`this machine cannot run headless steps; mesh decline <step>`);
- the kept message is missing (for example the daemon's store was reset) → `409`
  (`the offer's run data is not on this machine; ask @<by> to offer it again`);
- otherwise `HeadlessRuns.handleOffer(kept message, title)`, with the same `Idempotency-Key`
  (`offer-<message id>`); reply message `accepted <step>; running headless (run <runId>)` or
  `… queued (run <runId>)`.

The session-bound accepts (`mesh_accept` MCP tool, `mesh session accept`) keep refusing headless
offers (RL-21 `offer-headless`).

### CLI

- `mesh stop <step> <reason…>` → `POST /v1/steps/:id/stop-run`; prints
  `<step> parked: stopped: <reason>; asked <machine> to stop the run` (and `(machine offline: it stops when it is back)` when `online` is false).
- `mesh session stop <session> [--end] <reason…>` → resolves the session as `mesh say` does, then
  `POST /v1/sessions/:sid/stop` with `mode` `end` or `interrupt`.
- `mesh setup --remote-stop on|off` and `mesh setup --team-offers on|off` write `daemon.json`, as
  `--accept` does; `mesh status` prints both settings.
- A refusal prints the hub's `error` and exits 1.

### Notes for the client (mesh-ai row B-245)

Cross-repo coupling, recorded for the mesh-ai gateway and dashboard row; not hub behaviour:
- A team offer accepts no launch-time `mode` or `profile`: the hub answers `403 team-offer-override`.
  Only a step that declares its mode, or an interactive step, is routed to a teammate machine.
- A team offer waits for the machine owner's manual accept and expires after the hub's
  `offerExpiryMs` (30 minutes by default); the dashboard presents an expired team offer as
  expired, not as a fault.
- An approver who does not own the goal gets `403 not-approver` (stop-run) or `403 not-allowed`
  (session stop) through the relay and stops with their own login (REQ-STOPRIGHTS-1).

## Compatibility

- No migration (schema 12); every new field is OPTIONAL. No existing `error` text changes
  (REQ-COMPAT-1).
- Hub new, daemon old: the machine reports none of `runStop`, `remoteStop`, `teamOffers`, so
  stop-run answers `409 run-stop-unsupported`, session stop `409 stop-refused`, the machine is not
  listed by the team-machine read and an act-for team offer to it is `403 machine-not-yours`
  (REQ-COMPAT-2). Upgrade the hub first, then the daemons.
- Hub old, daemon new: the new facts are dropped by zod; no stop or team offer is ever made.
- A downgraded hub after a stop: `stoppedRun` and `team` are stripped on read; a parked step stays
  parked; a `finished` report with `killed_operator` is refused `400` by the old `RunExit` regex and
  the daemon stops retrying it (a `4xx`).
- A headless offer listed by a daemon older than this release was acked without being kept; after
  the upgrade `mesh accept` refuses it with "ask @<by> to offer it again", and the offerer re-offers
  it. Offers listed after the upgrade are kept (REQ-HLACCEPT-2).
- A daemon downgraded after it reported `runStop`, `remoteStop` or `teamOffers`: its next sync
  carries `machine` without them, the hub stores `false`, and no `run_stop`, `stop` or team offer
  reaches it (REQ-COMPAT-2).
- Own-token assigns to a teammate machine are unchanged (MM-3; auto by accept rules).
