# Spec RL-27: Stuck items of the team attention read carry the client's thresholds only

## Status
Draft (2026-11-26), revision 1.

Source: an external request (mesh-ai row B-241, the hub part of its AI-ops action item AIOPS-12, alert
rules). The client's owner answered the client's gate escalation on 2026-11-26 (mesh-ai
`reviews/owner/2026-11-26-aiops-decisions.md`, "Answers to the gate escalations", B-241 R1: "Yes,
attention only"): mesh-ai reads the stuck numbers of its alert rules (`since` and `turns`) only from the
team form of `GET /v1/attention`, and mesh-harness MUST return the instant a session entered `needs_you`
as `at` and MUST return stuck items in the team form with no hub-side threshold of its own. The client's
decision text is mesh-ai local ADR-0041, Amendment 2026-11-26, D9 "Cross-repo requirement on
mesh-harness", read with mesh-ai local ADR-0025 Amendment 2026-11-26 (AI-ops) D24.1 ("the stuck floors").
Where this spec and those texts differ, the client's ADRs govern what the client relies on; this spec
governs the hub.

Builds on RL-14 (`GET /v1/stuck`, its thresholds `needsYouMs` and `turns`, the shared stuck function) and
RL-22 (the team form `GET /v1/attention?team=1`, its kinds `stuck-session` and `stuck-step`, the turn that
reached the floor). Release: the first mesh-harness release after 0.1.43. Owner constraint carried over:
nothing tied to costs.

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "MAY" and
"OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 (RFC 2119, RFC 8174) when,
and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.

## Problem

mesh-ai's alert rules `session-stuck` (time stuck, seed 30 min) and `session-stuck-turns` (turns since
the last commit, seed 40) evaluate the `stuck-session` and `stuck-step` items of the team attention read
and nothing else: the client makes no `GET /v1/stuck`, `/v1/live` or `/v1/steps` read for them
(mesh-ai ADR-0025 REQ-HUB-36). The client sends the lowest threshold of its enabled rules as the read's
`needsYouMs` and `turns`, ages each item from its `at`, and compares `turns` with its own thresholds.
Two things make an alert wrong if the hub does not hold them:

1. **The age of a stuck session.** The owner's "stuck 30 min" is time stuck. If a `stuck-session` item's
   `at` were the instant the hub's stuck rule first held (entry instant + floor), the read time, or a
   fallback such as the session's last stop, the client would age the session from the wrong instant: a
   rule would fire one floor late, or at once on a session that just entered `needs_you`.
2. **A threshold of the hub's own.** If the hub left out a stuck item that meets the floor the caller
   sent (a hub default, a configured value, a stricter rule), a client rule below that hidden threshold
   would fire late or never.

The 0.1.43 code meets both: `stuckRows` (src/hub/service.ts:5022) skips a session with no `stateSince`,
returns a session whose `now − stateSince` is at least `needsYouMs` and a step whose turns since its last
commit are at least `turns`, and `teamAttention` (src/hub/service.ts:5076) gives a `stuck-session` the
`at` `stateSince` and `forMs`, and a `stuck-step` the full `turns` count. `stateSince` is the instant the
session entered its current state (src/core/state-time.ts:68). But no requirement binds this for the
team form: RL-22 states only that the team form uses the `GET /v1/stuck` rules, and the `needs-you-session`
kind in the same method falls back to `lastStopAt ?? lastEventAt` for its `at`. A later change to the
shared stuck function, or a shared `at` rule, would change the client's alerts with no test failing.

## Terminology

- **Team form**: `GET /v1/attention?team=1` (RL-22).
- **Floor**: the read's `needsYouMs` (for `stuck-session`) or `turns` (for `stuck-step`) value, sent by
  the caller or, when omitted, the RL-14 default (1800000 ms, 40 turns).
- **Entry instant**: a session's `stateSince` while its state is `needs_you`: the instant it entered
  `needs_you` (unchanged while it stays there, src/core/state-time.ts:68-70).
- **Turns since the last commit**: the count `turnsSinceCommit` gives (RL-14).

## Scope

In:
- The meaning of `at`, `forMs` and `turns` on `stuck-session` and `stuck-step` items of the team form,
  bound as requirements.
- The rule that the floors are the only stuck thresholds of the team form.
- Tests that pin both, and one docs sentence.

Out:
- `GET /v1/stuck` (RL-14), the personal form of `GET /v1/attention` and every other team kind: unchanged.
- The ranges and defaults of `needsYouMs` and `turns` (60000 to 604800000 ms, default 1800000; 1 to
  10000, default 40): unchanged. The client bounds its own thresholds at the same lowest values (mesh-ai
  ADR-0041 D7: a `session-stuck` threshold of at least 60 s, a `session-stuck-turns` threshold of at
  least 1 turn), so the lowest accepted floor never exceeds a client threshold.
- A new query input, field, kind, scope, `/healthz` feature, event, migration or daemon change: none.
- The `stuck-step` `at` rule (the start of the turn that reached the floor, RL-22): unchanged.

## Requirements

- **REQ-STUCKATT-1** In the team form, the `at` of a `stuck-session` item MUST be the session's entry
  instant (`stateSince`). It MUST NOT be the instant the floor was reached, the read time, the session's
  `lastStopAt`, `lastEventAt` or any other fallback.
- **REQ-STUCKATT-2** A live session in `needs_you` whose `stateSince` the hub does not hold MUST NOT be
  returned as a `stuck-session` item (it stays a `needs-you-session` item, RL-22).
- **REQ-STUCKATT-3** A `stuck-session` item MUST carry `forMs` = `generatedAt − at`, and a `stuck-step`
  item MUST carry `turns` = the step's turns since its last commit, not capped at the floor.
- **REQ-STUCKATT-4** The floors MUST be the only stuck thresholds of the team form: every live session
  holding a step of an open goal, in `needs_you` with `forMs ≥ needsYouMs`, MUST be returned as a
  `stuck-session` item, and every `claimed` step of an open goal with turns since its last commit
  `≥ turns` MUST be returned as a `stuck-step` item, the boundary included. No hub default (when the
  input is sent), hub configuration, environment variable or other rule MAY leave out such an item or
  raise the floor, except the `limit` cut of RL-22 (reported by `truncated`).
- **REQ-STUCKATT-5** The answer's `thresholds` MUST echo the floors applied: the values sent, else the
  RL-14 defaults.
- **REQ-STUCKATT-6** The change MUST be backwards compatible: no route, input, field or kind of the team
  form, the personal form or `GET /v1/stuck` is removed, renamed or given a new meaning; a client that
  omits a floor keeps the RL-14 default for it; the personal-form fixture of RL-22 AC-13 still matches.

## Interface contracts

No wire change. The team form's request, answer shape (`TeamAttention`, src/protocol/hub.ts) and codes
are RL-22's. This spec fixes the two stuck rows of RL-22's "Which items" table as follows (bold: bound
here):

| kind | Open while | `at` | Extra fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stuck-session` | a live session holding a `claimed` step of an open goal is in `needs_you` and **`generatedAt − stateSince ≥ needsYouMs`**; **a session without `stateSince` is never one** | **`stateSince`, the entry instant; no fallback** | `sid`, `machine`, **`forMs` = `generatedAt − at`** |
| `stuck-step` | a `claimed` step of an open goal has **turns since its last commit `≥ turns`** | the start of the turn that reached the floor (RL-22) | **`turns`: the full count, not capped**, `holder` |

The hub applies the floor and nothing else. The client applies its own thresholds over the items it
receives (mesh-ai ADR-0041 D9): a rule at or above the floor sees every item it needs.

### Why no new input

Three other shapes were considered and rejected:

- **No floor when the caller omits one.** It would return every `needs_you` session and every step with
  one turn since its last commit to RL-22 clients that omit the floors today (the deployed mesh-ai omits
  them while no stuck rule is enabled), a behaviour change for them (REQ-STUCKATT-6).
- **A lower `needsYouMs` minimum (0 instead of 60000).** It needs a `/healthz` feature so the client
  knows it can send less than 60000 (an older hub answers `400 bad-query`, which fails the whole read).
  The client instead bounds its thresholds at 60 s and 1 turn (mesh-ai ADR-0041 D7), which removes the
  need.
- **A `since` field beside `at`.** `at` already is the entry instant (REQ-STUCKATT-1); a second field
  with the same value adds nothing.

### Docs

- docs/guide/auth.md, the "Team reads" paragraph: after "stuck steps and sessions (the `GET /v1/stuck`
  rules at `needsYouMs` and `turns`)" add: "; those two values are the only stuck thresholds (an item
  exactly at a floor is returned), a stuck session's `at` is the instant it entered needs_you and its
  `forMs` the time since, and a stuck step's `turns` is its full count since its last commit (RL-27)".

## Compatibility

Nothing changes on the wire, in the store or in the daemon. A hub of this release answers a client of
RL-22 exactly as 0.1.43 does for the same state. If a test of this spec fails against 0.1.43, the fix is
in `stuckRows` or `teamAttention` (src/hub/service.ts) and MUST keep REQ-STUCKATT-6. The client relies on
this spec from mesh-ai row B-241's deploy (mesh-ai ADR-0041 REQ-SETLOG-13): it records the mesh-harness
release that carries RL-27 and its test result.

## Acceptance criteria

Tests in `test/team-attention-rl27.test.ts` (the `Ak` kit of `test/helpers/attention-kit.ts`; person
`ann`; the service token `reader` with `goals:read`; a clock the test moves) and `test/docs-rl27.test.ts`.

- **AC-01** (REQ-STUCKATT-1, -3) A session `s-1` holding step `a`, in `needs_you` with `stateSince = T0`,
  `lastStopAt = T0 + 20 min` and `lastEventAt = T0 + 25 min`; at `T0 + 31 min` the team form with
  `needsYouMs=1800000` returns one `stuck-session` for `s-1` with `at = T0` and `forMs = 31 min`, not
  `T0 + 20 min`, `T0 + 25 min` or `T0 + 30 min`. Unit, src/core/state-time.ts: a second `needs_you`
  report (another `needs`) keeps `stateSince = T0`.
- **AC-02** (REQ-STUCKATT-1) The same session read with `needsYouMs=60000` and with `needsYouMs=1800000`
  at the same instant: both items have the same `at` (`T0`); the floor changes which items exist, never
  their `at`.
- **AC-03** (REQ-STUCKATT-2) A live session in `needs_you` holding a step, written without `stateSince`:
  no `stuck-session` item at any floor; its `needs-you-session` item is still returned.
- **AC-04** (REQ-STUCKATT-4) Boundaries: sessions in `needs_you` for exactly 60000 ms and 59999 ms with
  `needsYouMs=60000`: the first is returned, the second is not; steps with exactly 5 and 4 turns since
  their last commit with `turns=5`: the first is returned, the second is not.
- **AC-05** (REQ-STUCKATT-3) A step with 47 turns since its last commit read with `turns=1`, `turns=40` and
  `turns=47`: each read returns it with `turns: 47`; its `at` is the start of the 1st, the 40th and the
  47th turn after the commit (RL-22 rule).
- **AC-06** (REQ-STUCKATT-4, -5) With `needsYouMs=60000&turns=1`, every `needs_you` session holding a step
  for at least 1 min and every claimed step with at least one turn since its last commit is returned
  (a world of 3 sessions and 3 steps, all returned); `thresholds` is `{ needsYouMs: 60000, turns: 1 }`;
  without the two inputs `thresholds` is `{ needsYouMs: 1800000, turns: 40 }` and only the items at or
  above those values are returned.
- **AC-07** (REQ-STUCKATT-4) A session of a trial goal, and a session holding a step of a `done` goal, are
  not returned (open goals only, RL-22); with `trial=include` the trial one is.
- **AC-08** (REQ-STUCKATT-6) The RL-22 tests `test/team-attention.test.ts` (AC-10 to AC-13, including the
  0.1.41 personal-form fixture) and the RL-14 `GET /v1/stuck` tests pass unchanged.
- **AC-09** `test/docs-rl27.test.ts`: docs/guide/auth.md holds the sentence of §Docs ("only stuck
  thresholds", "the instant it entered needs_you", "full count").
- **AC-10** `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm lint` green.

## Open questions

None. The case where the client sends no `turns` (no `session-stuck-turns` rule enabled), so the RL-14
default of 40 turns decides which steps are `stuck-step` items, is the client's, recorded in mesh-ai
ADR-0041 (Amendment 2026-11-26, D9 and Consequences); this spec keeps the default for backwards
compatibility (REQ-STUCKATT-6).
